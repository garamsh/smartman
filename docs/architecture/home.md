# Home

The app's `home/` shell: the signed-in screens that show more than one domain or lead from one domain into another, and how they compose the domains.

## Current decisions

### Surface and location

- The shell lives in `app/lib/home/`; its surface is `app/lib/home/home.dart`, which exports `HomeScreen`, with internals under `app/lib/home/src/` ([ADR 0039](adr/0039-name-every-top-level-folder-by-its-responsibility-compose-across-domains-in-a-home-shell-and-keep-seams-off-screens.md), [`structure.md`](structure.md)):
  - `home_screen.dart` — `HomeScreen`: the three tabs and their bar, or the first-run home before the first deck, with the photo import bar and the offer to resume a photo import;
  - `first_run.dart` — `FirstRun`, the layout of how to start that the first-run home and the cardless 오늘 tab share;
  - `today_tab.dart` — the 오늘 tab: the today, done and backlog cards, the last seven study days and today's decks;
  - `decks_tab.dart` — the 덱 tab: the deck rows, their progress and today chips, the search, and the buttons under the rows;
  - `record_tab.dart` — the 기록 tab: its title over `study`'s record;
  - `deck_screen.dart` — `DeckScreen`, a deck's page;
  - `new_deck_sheet.dart` and `option_sheet.dart` — the new-deck sheet and the sheet of options it and "카드 추가" open;
  - `progress_bar.dart` and `hero.dart` — the progress bar, and the today cards' large count;
  - `mascot.dart` — `Mascot`, the brand's symbol in its theme's colour, alone or centred in a circle and hidden from screen readers, which only the shell's screens draw ([`design.md`](design.md)).
- The composition site passes `HomeScreen` to `account`'s `AccountGate` as the screen it opens while an account is signed in ([`account.md`](account.md)). Nothing else imports `home/`.

### Composition

- `home/` sits above every domain: it imports the domains' surfaces and the foundations, and builds each screen from the domains' exported widgets, handing values and callbacks between them, as a web route does.
- It holds no reads and no rules of its own: it declares no provider and imports no database. What a screen shows, it reads through a domain's providers and widgets.
- The tabs compose:
  - `deck`'s `decksProvider`, `cardCountsProvider`, `DeckRow`, `DeckTile`, `showNameSheet`, `showDeckAppearanceSheet` and `DeckRepository`, for the decks, their rows and a new deck ([`deck.md`](deck.md));
  - `study`'s `TodayOverviewReader`, for the 오늘 tab's count, goal, streak, next review and last seven study days; `TodayCounter`, for each deck's count; `ProgressCounter`, for each deck's progress; `StatsView`, the 기록 tab's content, given the tab's title; `openStudy`, through which every study button opens a `StudyScreen`; and `DailyMinutesButton`, `FirstDailyMinutes` and `DailyMinutesRow`, the daily study minutes' control, first-run step and account row ([`study.md`](study.md), [ADR 0086](adr/0086-show-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-on-the-today-card-the-completion-card-a-one-time-first-run-step-the-account-screen-and-the-web-s-today-page.md));
  - `account`'s `AccountAvatar` on the account button, and `AccountScreen`, the settings screen, given `sync`'s `SyncStatusView` as the status it shows and, as its study settings' rows, `study`'s `DailyMinutesRow`, `study`'s `AutoplayAudioRow` and `deck`'s `PartOfSpeechPresetsRow` ([`account.md`](account.md), [`sync.md`](sync.md));
  - `sharing`'s `sharingServiceProvider`, for the browse screen ([`sharing.md`](sharing.md));
  - `photo_import`'s `photoImportProvider`, for the photo options, and `photoImportsProvider`, `PhotoImportBar`, `openPhotoImport` and `offerPhotoImportResume`, for the import under way ([`ai-extraction.md`](ai-extraction.md)).
- The deck screen composes `deck`'s `deckProvider`, `cardsProvider`, `DeckTile`, `CardBackRow`, `CardEditorScreen`, `showNameSheet`, `showDeckAppearanceSheet` and `DeckRepository`; `study`'s `TodayCounter`, `ProgressCounter` and `openStudy`; `sharing`'s `sharingServiceProvider` and `onlineProvider`, for publishing; and `photo_import`'s `photoImportProvider`.
- A `DeckRow` takes what it shows of today and of its progress as widgets, which the 덱 tab builds from `TodayCounter` and `ProgressCounter`: the deck's places for what `study` draws ([`structure.md`](structure.md)).

### Tabs

- The signed-in app is three tabs, 오늘, 덱 and 기록, in a bar at the foot on the surface over a hairline: Lucide's sun, layers and column chart over each name, the selected tab in ink and bold, the others muted, each read as a selectable button ([ADR 0036](adr/0036-organise-the-app-in-today-decks-and-record-tabs-and-define-the-daily-goal-forecast-and-card-progress.md)). The tabs keep their state while another is shown. Back on 덱 or 기록 returns to 오늘; on 오늘 it is left to the platform.
- While a photo import is under way, `photo_import`'s bar sits above the tab bar, or at the foot of the first-run home: "사진 읽는 중 k/n", "카드 n장 확인하기" or "읽던 사진 k장 이어서 읽기". A tap opens the import, and an import that saves opens the deck it saved into ([ADR 0094](adr/0094-read-a-photo-import-of-up-to-fifty-photos-three-at-a-time-as-an-app-level-job-kept-on-the-device-and-offered-again-after-a-restart.md)).
- On opening, when a closed app left a photo import with photos unread, the home screen offers to resume it, "읽던 사진 k장을 이어서 읽을까요?", once the import is read back.
- Before the first deck, the first-run home shows in place of the tabs, under the lockup and the account button: the mascot on its cream circle, "첫 덱을 만들어 볼까요?", one line on how to start, then "새 덱 만들기", the one primary, which opens the new-deck sheet, and the text "공개 덱 둘러보기" with sharing ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-start-a-deck-or-a-card-from-one-sheet-that-holds-the-photo-source-and-name-public-decks-once.md)).

### The decks tab

- The top bar holds "내 덱 n" at 26/800 alone ([ADR 0050](adr/0050-make-a-deck-from-a-button-under-the-deck-list-and-remove-the-deck-selection-mode.md)).
- A 48 px "덱 검색" keeps the decks whose name holds it, ignoring case.
- Each deck is a `DeckRow` ([`deck.md`](deck.md)), with a brand-tint "오늘 n" chip when a session over it would study n > 0 cards now, under its name "n장 중 m장 익힘", and under that a progress bar, the mastered cards in ink and the learning ones quieter. The learning count is not in the text: a screen reader reads the counts from the bar's label, "익힘 m장, 학습 중 l장, 새 카드 k장", in the row's node ([ADR 0096](adr/0096-state-a-deck-s-mastered-of-all-cards-under-its-name-over-its-bar-on-the-row-and-the-deck-page-and-an-empty-deck-as-0-cards.md)). - Under the rows, "새 덱 만들기", an outlined button, then, with sharing, "공개 덱 둘러보기", a text button, which opens the browse screen, each its words alone ([ADR 0116](adr/0116-draw-an-icon-only-where-it-says-what-the-words-beside-it-do-not-or-follows-the-platform.md), [ADR 0050](adr/0050-make-a-deck-from-a-button-under-the-deck-list-and-remove-the-deck-selection-mode.md)).

A photo import that saved opens the deck it saved into.
- A tap opens a deck's screen, and a long-press does the same. There is no selection mode: 오늘's "모두 학습" studies every deck with cards today.

### The record tab

- The tab is `study`'s `StatsView` over every deck, given "기록" at 26/800, read as a heading, as the title it shows alone above its charts. The period selector sits in the retention card beside its heading, since it changes that chart alone and three charts left unchanged under a page-wide control read as a control not working (issue #317, the PM's answer there) ([`study.md`](study.md)). There is no Plus badge: gating waits for the subscription issue (issue #90).
- The tab is built the first time it is shown, not when the tabs open, since its read folds every review through the engine; after that it keeps its state as the other tabs do.
