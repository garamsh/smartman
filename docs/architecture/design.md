# Design

The app's design language: the principles every screen follows for usability and visual design together, where each is applied today, and the theme, tokens and components that carry them.

## Current decisions

### Principles

Every screen is designed to be simple, intuitive and easy to use. A new screen or a change to one follows these, and copies the idiom named under each.

1. **One primary action per screen.** It is the most prominent element and sits in thumb reach, at the bottom. Secondary actions are quieter or live in a menu.
   - Study: the current card, low on the screen, answered by a swipe, with "안내 다시 보기" alone under it and no answer button ([ADR 0099](adr/0099-answer-study-cards-by-swipe-alone-and-grade-the-previous-card-by-its-colour-alone-on-the-phone.md)); closing is a small icon at the top. On the completion card, "학습하기" is the one primary fill while new cards are left past today's, with "그만하기" a quiet text button below it; with none left, "그만하기" is the primary fill at the cta height under "더 학습할 카드가 없어요" ([ADR 0093](adr/0093-make-stopping-the-finished-completion-card-s-primary-and-show-the-resume-notice-in-the-progress-bar-s-place-on-the-phone.md)). The whole-card sheet is titled "카드", its "앞면" and "뒷면" two section labels of one rank, since neither side is the sheet's subject (#318).
   - Card editor: "저장하고 다음 카드" is the filled button in the bottom bar, "저장" outlined beside it; editing an existing card offers "저장" alone, and ends its form with "카드 삭제" in red text. "미리보기" is a text action in the top bar ([ADR 0117](adr/0117-draw-the-card-editor-as-one-group-per-side-with-a-full-screen-preview-and-every-action-in-words-or-a-row-menu.md)).
   - Card editor: one block list, with no kind to choose ([ADR 0114](adr/0114-write-every-card-as-a-free-block-list-remove-the-flip-cloze-and-table-forms-and-migrate-every-card-to-free.md)); "+ 뜻 추가" is ink-2 text at the end of its block; a 표 block has no mode: its toolbar of chips over the table acts on the selected cell, never a second segmented bar ([ADR 0102](adr/0102-edit-a-table-in-its-cells-with-a-toolbar-over-it-and-no-mode-switch.md)); a cloze's one action follows the caret, "빈칸 넣기" or, inside a blank, "빈칸 풀기", a small outlined chip right under its line and never a primary fill ([ADR 0117](adr/0117-draw-the-card-editor-as-one-group-per-side-with-a-full-screen-preview-and-every-action-in-words-or-a-row-menu.md)), with no second row repeating the blanks; and a cloze blank in its line and a chosen table cell are drawn in `brandTint`, as every blank is (issue #313, [ADR 0101](adr/0101-edit-a-cloze-as-one-face-with-its-blanks-as-blocks-in-the-line-and-a-single-insert-blank-action.md)); the empty blank "빈칸 넣기" just opened is drawn in its line as a hidden blank is, a gap over a `muted` underline ending at the caret (issue #483). Each side is one white group under its muted name, its blocks rows parted by hairlines, its fields borderless until focused, and it ends with a quiet "+ 추가" in `muted` ([ADR 0108](adr/0108-add-a-free-template-and-an-add-control-that-puts-any-block-on-either-side-of-every-template.md), [ADR 0117](adr/0117-draw-the-card-editor-as-one-group-per-side-with-a-full-screen-preview-and-every-action-in-words-or-a-row-menu.md)); its sheet, "앞면에 추가" or "뒷면에 추가", lists the block types in one grouped list with no icon, each its name and, but 글, one line; a 글 is its field, and every other block is its type's name in `muted` over its fields, with no icon, each block with a ⋯ menu at the end of its row. While a sense's label field has focus, the user's labels sit in a strip right above the keyboard, the field's own tinted `brandTint`.
   - 오늘 tab: "모두 학습" on the today card or the backlog card, or "학습하기" at the end of the done card, as the one navy fill, always inside the card it acts on ([ADR 0120](adr/0120-state-today-s-progress-once-put-every-study-button-in-its-card-name-the-record-plainly-and-draw-photo-import-s-bar-checks-and-moves-as-the-other-screens-do.md)).
   - 덱 tab: "새 덱 만들기", an outlined button under the rows, before the text button "공개 덱 둘러보기" ([ADR 0116](adr/0116-draw-an-icon-only-where-it-says-what-the-words-beside-it-do-not-or-follows-the-platform.md)), its ways in a sheet that also holds the photo's source; first-run home: "새 덱 만들기", which opens the same sheet; and nothing in the top bar but the title; deck screen: "학습하기" on the deck's "오늘 학습" card and "카드 추가" in the bottom bar, or, while the deck holds no card, "카드 추가" alone as the primary ([ADR 0066](adr/0066-open-a-deck-made-by-hand-and-lead-an-empty-deck-and-a-cardless-today-tab-to-adding-cards.md)), `BottomActions`; rename and delete sit in the deck screen's menu.
   - Every sheet: `AppSheet` pins its actions at the bottom, full width, with the primary one last; "새 덱" and "이름 바꾸기" have one field and one primary, "만들기" or "저장".
   - Daily study minutes: the first-run sheet's one primary is "저장", with its quiet "건너뛰기" above it, and the settings screen's "저장" is pinned at its foot ([ADR 0091](adr/0091-draw-the-today-card-as-a-progress-bar-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-only-in-settings.md)); the minutes are one large number in the figure style between a − and a +, typed in place on the number keypad, with no choice to pick from ([ADR 0090](adr/0090-pick-the-daily-study-minutes-as-one-large-number-between-minus-and-plus-typed-in-place-with-two-tiles-of-what-they-mean.md), [ADR 0086](adr/0086-show-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-on-the-today-card-the-completion-card-a-one-time-first-run-step-the-account-screen-and-the-web-s-today-page.md)).
   - Sign-in: the stacked logo and its two lines sit on the ground's panel in the upper part, and Google's button, with the quieter Apple slot and the terms below it, at the bottom.
   - Every screen: `AppButton`'s variants rank the actions, one primary fill per screen or sheet.
2. **Content over chrome.** No label, hint or heading restates what the screen already shows. Remove before adding.
   - Study: no deck name and no hint text, but for the empty previous card's place's one line, "응답한 카드가 여기에 쌓여요", the 3 s notice that a resumed day goes on, and the first-use coach, which teaches on the cards themselves and is replayed only on request ([ADR 0064](adr/0064-teach-study-with-a-first-use-finger-coach-and-show-the-day-s-last-card-and-a-brief-notice-when-a-day-resumes.md)); the completion card carries no sentence beyond its heading and one line of counts, and "더 학습할 카드가 없어요" in place of "학습하기" where no new card is left; studying past today's cards is not marked apart ([ADR 0049](adr/0049-study-in-one-flow-that-goes-on-past-today-s-cards-as-recorded-study-with-the-current-card-showing-its-front-alone.md)).
   - Deck screen: each row shows its front alone, and its back muted after it while the "뒷면 보기" switch is on, with nothing drawn for a hidden back; the deck name is the only title, the muted line under it the screen's one count of its cards, and the row above the cards the section label "카드" ([ADR 0118](adr/0118-name-each-concept-once-write-each-role-in-one-form-hold-every-screen-to-one-set-of-visual-details-and-redraw-settings-and-the-deck-screen-by-them.md)).
   - Tabs: no line explains a long-press or a tab; 기록 has no explanatory paragraph, only each chart's heading, its figures and a line beside the heading.
   - Every sheet: `AppSheet` has a title and at most one or two sentences of body; `AppTextField` shows its label once, above the field.
   - Daily study minutes: the settings row says only "하루 학습 시간" and the minutes, and the picker one sentence, with its two tiles saying what the choice means rather than how it is computed.
3. **Direct manipulation where it is natural.** Swipe to answer, tap to toggle. Every gesture has an accessible alternative: a semantics action or a visible control.
   - Study: on the phone a horizontal swipe answers, with the custom actions "안다" and "모른다" for a screen reader and no answer button ([ADR 0099](adr/0099-answer-study-cards-by-swipe-alone-and-grade-the-previous-card-by-its-colour-alone-on-the-phone.md)), and on the web a touch swipe answers the same, with the buttons and the arrow keys ([ADR 0084](adr/0084-answer-the-web-s-current-study-card-by-a-touch-swipe-as-the-phone-does.md)); a horizontal drag on the previous card changes its answer, with the custom action "안다로 바꾸기" or "모른다로 바꾸기", and a tap on it opens the whole card; a drag on either card moves that card alone.
   - Deck screen: a tap on a card row opens the card in the editor, and a screen reader hears the row as one button with the hint "카드 편집" ([ADR 0118](adr/0118-name-each-concept-once-write-each-role-in-one-form-hold-every-screen-to-one-set-of-visual-details-and-redraw-settings-and-the-deck-screen-by-them.md)).
   - Card editor: a listed block moves and is deleted through its ⋯ menu, "위로", "아래로" and a red "삭제", not a drag, the menu named for a screen reader by its side and place ([ADR 0108](adr/0108-add-a-free-template-and-an-add-control-that-puts-any-block-on-either-side-of-every-template.md)).
  - Card editor: a 뜻 목록's senses move and are deleted through each sense's ⋯ menu, of the same items, not a drag, and a label is typed in its own chip; each menu is named for a screen reader ([ADR 0100](adr/0100-merge-the-word-kind-into-basic-type-each-sense-s-label-and-offer-the-user-s-labels-above-the-keyboard.md), [ADR 0117](adr/0117-draw-the-card-editor-as-one-group-per-side-with-a-full-screen-preview-and-every-action-in-words-or-a-row-menu.md)).
   - 라벨: a preset moves by a drag on its handle or, without one, by its ⋯ menu of "위로" and "아래로", which also deletes it, and is edited in place by a tap; each control but the handle, which a screen reader skips, is named for a screen reader ([ADR 0100](adr/0100-merge-the-word-kind-into-basic-type-each-sense-s-label-and-offer-the-user-s-labels-above-the-keyboard.md), [ADR 0117](adr/0117-draw-the-card-editor-as-one-group-per-side-with-a-full-screen-preview-and-every-action-in-words-or-a-row-menu.md)).
   - Photo import list: a swipe left leaves a row out, and its check, which a screen reader hears and taps, leaves it out or puts it back; a tap opens the row's edit sheet, and a screen reader taps the same. In the edit sheet a meaning is kept by the same round check and moves through its ⋯ menu, "위로" and "아래로", as the card editor's does, not a drag ([ADR 0120](adr/0120-state-today-s-progress-once-put-every-study-button-in-its-card-name-the-record-plainly-and-draw-photo-import-s-bar-checks-and-moves-as-the-other-screens-do.md)).
   - Every sheet: a drag or a tap outside dismisses it, and Android's back does the same.
4. **Never wait.** Act at once and sync in the background. Show progress only for work the user asked for and must wait on, such as photo extraction.
   - Study: the next card is on screen before the answer's write finishes.
   - Photo import: extraction shows the photo, a progress bar and skeleton rows, and can be cancelled.
   - Photo import of several photos: one screen states "사진 n장 중 k장 읽음" as a heading a screen reader hears as it changes, over a bar of the same k photos read, and each photo's row with its thumbnail and its state in words beside an icon, so colour never carries it alone; a failure shows on its row with its actions, and the other photos go on ([ADR 0089](adr/0089-import-up-to-ten-photos-into-one-list-read-two-at-a-time-and-state-today-s-limit-before-reading.md)).
   - Photo import: the photos go on being read while the user leaves the screen; a bar above the tab bar, one button a screen reader hears as it changes, states "사진 읽는 중 k/n" over a bar of the k photos read, then "카드 n장 확인하기", and leads back to the import; a closed app's import is offered again on the next open, and its photos already read are not read again ([ADR 0094](adr/0094-read-a-photo-import-of-up-to-fifty-photos-three-at-a-time-as-an-app-level-job-kept-on-the-device-and-offered-again-after-a-restart.md)).
   - Photo import: a photo's thumbnail opens it full screen, a button named "사진 i 크게 보기"; the viewer's swipes across, swipe down and pinch have its close, the system back and the thumbnails' order as alternatives.
   - Photo import list of several photos: each photo's group is headed by its thumbnail, "사진 i" and its row count, read as a heading and a button whose state is expanded or collapsed, at least a tap target high; a tap collapses or expands it.
   - Settings: a theme choice applies at once and is stored after.
   - Daily study minutes: "저장" stores them on the device, sync pushes them after, and the counts behind the sheet are read again as it closes; the preview moves with each choice before anything is stored.
   - Photo import: a swipe removes or puts back a card at once, and a topic's switch drops or keeps its cards at once; nothing is written until the cards are added.
   - Settings and photo import: `AppButton`'s loading state shows progress in the button the user pressed, and the button cannot be pressed twice.
5. **Correct, don't confirm.** Prefer undo or a toggle to a confirmation sheet. Confirm only what cannot be undone: deleting a deck, a card with its study history, or the account, and discarding a photo import that holds cards, whose photos cost today's limit again to read.
   - Study: any answer of the session is corrected by a drag on the centred card, or its action, with no confirmation.
   - Card editor: a duplicate front opens a sheet that offers to add anyway or open the existing card, and is dismissed to do neither; nothing is overwritten.
   - Photo import: the top bar's × and the retake in its ⋯ on an import that would save a card are confirmed through `showAppConfirm`, "찾은 카드를 버릴까요?", with "버리기" last; the system back only leaves the screen, and the import is kept.
   - Deck screen, card editor, account and preview: deleting a deck, deleting a card, signing out with unsynced changes, deleting the account, blocking a user and leaving the card editor with changes, whose cancel reads "계속 쓰기", are confirmed through `showAppConfirm`, which states the consequence in one sentence above a cancel and, last, a red final button.
6. **One visual language.** Theme tokens only; the split card, front left and back right, each half's content centred across and down and shrunk toward a floor until its widest word fits, so a word never breaks inside itself while the floor fits, wherever a card shows both sides, and the study's current card, its front alone, centred and shrunk the same way ([ADR 0049](adr/0049-study-in-one-flow-that-goes-on-past-today-s-cards-as-recorded-study-with-the-current-card-showing-its-front-alone.md)); rounded surfaces on the ground colour; 44 pt minimum targets.
   - Study's previous card and the duplicate sheet: the same split card, each half centred; `deck`'s `SplitCard` lays out the halves for the previous card, the card preview and the card rows ([`deck.md`](deck.md)). The card editor shows no card of its own: its "미리보기" draws the card as study's current card does, its front and, a tap later, its back, or its one face with its blanks empty and then filled ([ADR 0117](adr/0117-draw-the-card-editor-as-one-group-per-side-with-a-full-screen-preview-and-every-action-in-words-or-a-row-menu.md)).
   - A table or cloze card is drawn whole wherever a card is drawn split: its front across the card through `deck`'s `WholeCardView`, drawn from its blocks: a text block as its title, in the headline on a card and, in a list row's smaller style, that style in bold, a cloze centred and fitted as a front is, a table in the body size inside `outline` lines with its header row on `ground` and its header row and each row's first cell bold; an empty blank is a gap of its answer's width over a `muted` underline, its answer not drawn, and a filled one the answer on `brandTint` ([ADR 0068](adr/0068-store-a-table-or-cloze-card-as-typed-content-blocks-and-draw-it-whole-its-blanks-filled-in-place-once-answered.md), [ADR 0119](adr/0119-draw-every-screen-from-one-radius-scale-one-chip-three-type-sizes-a-press-dim-and-three-haptics-and-a-hidden-blank-as-an-empty-underlined-gap.md)). A photo card is drawn whole the same way: the photo whole, its hidden boxes filled in `hero`, a revealed one outlined in 2 px of `brand` with its answer on `brandTint` at its top left ([ADR 0109](adr/0109-store-an-image-block-s-boxes-in-fractions-of-the-image-and-draw-them-with-the-image-s-one-transform.md)).
   - A vocabulary sense reads the same from every book: each label in brackets, a part of speech by its fixed short form, as `[명]`, another label as printed, then the meaning, one sense per line. The label is its own span with a gap of one spacing step, `AppSpace.s4` (`mr-1` on the web), narrower than a word space, after it; a sense with no label shows its meaning alone ([ADR 0075](adr/0075-store-a-vocabulary-card-s-senses-as-a-typed-block-and-show-each-label-in-brackets-before-its-meaning.md)).
   - 덱 tab, 오늘 tab and deck screen: rows on rounded surfaces over the ground; the white cards over them, with the card shadow.
   - Every screen and sheet: the tokens below and the components that use them.
   - At a larger system text size nothing is cut (issue #295):
     - a box sized for its text grows with it: the tab bar from its 64 by the text scale;
     - a chip's label wraps inside the chip;
     - a figure or label a narrow column holds, the day bars' and the tab bar's among them, stays on one line and shrinks to the column only where it is wider, so a word never breaks inside itself.
7. **Meaningful motion** that shows where things go. Reduce motion is respected.
   - Study: an answered card flies off and rises into the previous card's place while the next emerges from below; under reduce motion they cross-fade. A re-decided card springs back into place, at once under reduce motion.
   - Photo import list: a row swiped left follows the finger and springs back into place, left out.
   - Card editor's 미리보기: a tap turns the card over about its vertical axis in 220 ms; under reduce motion it switches at once ([ADR 0117](adr/0117-draw-the-card-editor-as-one-group-per-side-with-a-full-screen-preview-and-every-action-in-words-or-a-row-menu.md)).
   - Study's coach: the card moves out with an ease, holds and springs back under a finger; under reduce motion each step is a still image, the card held out ([ADR 0064](adr/0064-teach-study-with-a-first-use-finger-coach-and-show-the-day-s-last-card-and-a-brief-notice-when-a-day-resumes.md)). The notice that a day goes on slides in and out, or fades.
   - Every sheet: `showAppSheet` slides it up with the platform's sheet motion, and shows it at once under reduce motion.
   - Every button and row: a press dims it at once, with no splash and no scale, so reduce motion asks nothing of it; a light haptic marks answering a card, saving one in the editor and confirming through `showAppConfirm`, and no other press ([ADR 0119](adr/0119-draw-every-screen-from-one-radius-scale-one-chip-three-type-sizes-a-press-dim-and-three-haptics-and-a-hidden-blank-as-an-empty-underlined-gap.md)).
8. **Plain-language states.** Empty, offline and error states say what happened and what to do next, in one or two short sentences, in the voice below.
   - First-run home, a cardless 오늘 tab and deck screen: an empty list says how to start, and leads there with its one primary, and a search with no match, on the 덱 tab or the deck screen, says to try other words.
   - 오늘 tab and deck screen: in a backlog, one sentence says new cards wait until the reviews are caught up ([`study.md`](study.md)).
   - Study: with no card left and none answered today, one sentence says there is nothing to study today.
   - 기록: a chart with nothing to show says so in one sentence: no review in the period, no card reviewed, no card mastered.
   - Every screen: a storage failure says what failed and to try again.
   - Browse: offline, it says the public decks appear once connected; the deck menu says why publishing is disabled.

### Voice

Every string is written as one writer would write it ([ADR 0024](adr/0024-one-voice-and-a-sheet-action-rule.md)).

- Korean, in `app/lib/l10n/app_ko.arb`, is 해요체 throughout: plain, short, and consistent in its sentence endings ("아직 덱이 없어요.", "카드 3장을 만들었어요"). No sentence ends in 합쇼체 ("…ㅂ니다", "…ㅂ니까"). A label or a button names its action ("로그아웃", "그래도 추가").
- English, in `app_en.arb`, matches it in tone: plain, second person, no exclamation marks.
- Which word names a concept, and which form a string of each role takes, is §Copy.

### Components

### Details

What keeps a screen calm rather than decorated ([ADR 0118](adr/0118-name-each-concept-once-write-each-role-in-one-form-hold-every-screen-to-one-set-of-visual-details-and-redraw-settings-and-the-deck-screen-by-them.md), issue #454). A screen redesigned from now on follows every item; one not yet redesigned is brought to them when it is. The settings screen and the deck screen follow them today, on the phone and as the web's settings and deck pages ([`web.md`](web.md)), and on the phone the 오늘 and 기록 tabs and photo import ([ADR 0120](adr/0120-state-today-s-progress-once-put-every-study-button-in-its-card-name-the-record-plainly-and-draw-photo-import-s-bar-checks-and-moves-as-the-other-screens-do.md)), and every screen draws the radius scale, the one chip, the three sizes, the press and the haptics through the shared layer ([ADR 0119](adr/0119-draw-every-screen-from-one-radius-scale-one-chip-three-type-sizes-a-press-dim-and-three-haptics-and-a-hidden-blank-as-an-empty-underlined-gap.md)).

- **Structure.**
  - One white group per meaning: a side of a card, a settings section, a list. Inside a group a hairline separates the rows, never a border on each field; a field in a group has no border until it is focused, then a 1.5 px ink outline and no shadow; `AppTextField` draws its 2 px ring until the card editor's redesign brings it to that.
  - One primary, the navy fill, per screen, always in the same place: the bottom bar, or inside the card it acts on. A secondary action is outlined; a tertiary one is plain text in `ink2`, such as "+ 추가".
  - A destructive action is red text at the end of a screen or inside ⋯, never a red fill outside a confirmation.
- **Shape and depth.**
  - One radius scale: group 14, button 12, chip and label 6, sheet 22, the `AppRadius` tokens of the same names.
  - A shadow only on what floats: the study card, the preview card and the sheets; never on a group or a row.
- **Type.**
  - Pretendard, at most three sizes per screen: a title, the body and a caption. A button's label is the body size. The count is of the screen's own chrome: a large figure with its unit, a card's own content on the study and preview cards, and Google's button are outside it. A sheet is counted apart from the screen under it.
  - A large figure in `figure`, about −2% tracking and tabular digits, its unit, such as 장 or 분, right after it, smaller and muted.
  - Korean breaks only between words everywhere, through `keepWords` in `design/word_break.dart`, every card surface included; on the web through `word-break: keep-all` ([ADR 0123](adr/0123-step-a-long-card-side-down-through-three-sizes-then-scroll-it-inside-a-card-that-keeps-its-size.md)).
  - A card's text that does not fit its card at the largest size steps down through the card's three sizes, then scrolls inside the card with a fading bottom edge; it is never cut on the current card or the preview, and the card keeps its size (`CardText` above, [ADR 0123](adr/0123-step-a-long-card-side-down-through-three-sizes-then-scroll-it-inside-a-card-that-keeps-its-size.md)).
- **Colour.**
  - Navy is the only accent. Green and red mean 안다 and 모른다 alone; a selection, sync and a photo read take navy or the word alone, as the photo import deck chooser's check and a read photo's check do.
  - Hidden content shows as nothing, never as a grey bar that reads as loading: an empty blank is a gap over an underline.
- **Motion and touch**, each skipped when the system asks to reduce motion.
  - The preview and study cards flip in 3D in 200 to 250 ms. A pressed row or button dims a little, under its text's colour at 10%, with no splash and no scale bounce; the dim is not motion, so it stays under reduce motion.
  - A light haptic on answering a card, on saving a card, and on a destructive confirmation, `showAppConfirm`'s red button, alone.
- **Icons** are §Brand's rule ([ADR 0116](adr/0116-draw-an-icon-only-where-it-says-what-the-words-beside-it-do-not-or-follows-the-platform.md)).
- **Never:** a gradient, glass blur, or an emoji or illustration as decoration; an icon in a tinted square; a second chip style; everything centred; the same text in two places on one screen.
