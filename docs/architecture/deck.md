# Deck

The `deck` domain: decks, the cards inside them, card content, the card key, and the duplicate rule.

## Current decisions

### Surface and location

- The domain lives in `app/lib/deck/`; its public surface is `app/lib/deck/deck.dart`, with internals under `app/lib/deck/src/` ([ADR 0002](adr/0002-domain-public-surface-and-shared-database.md)).
- The surface exports:
  - the model: `Deck`, `Card`, `DeckId`, `CardId`, `CardSource`, `DeckVisibility` with `PrivateDeck` and `PublicDeck`, and `DeckSelection`, a set of decks that cannot be empty;
  - content: `Content`, `TextContent`, `ClozeContent` with `clozeBlank`, `TableContent` with `TableBlank`, `SensesContent` with `CardSense`, `SenseLabel`, `PartOfSpeechLabel`, `OtherLabel` and `PartOfSpeech`, `ImageContent` with `ImageBox` ([ADR 0109](adr/0109-store-an-image-block-s-boxes-in-fractions-of-the-image-and-draw-them-with-the-image-s-one-transform.md)), `AudioContent` with `audioOf`, a side's sounds ([ADR 0112](adr/0112-store-an-audio-block-by-media-id-and-length-up-to-60-seconds-and-play-the-front-s-sound-once-as-a-study-card-appears.md)), `UnsupportedContent`, `decodeContent`, which reads the stored encoding, `decodeSense`, which reads one sense as the block and the extraction function write it, `plainText`, a side's text with its blanks filled, `oneFaceOf`, the face a card is studied on, and `isWholeCard`, `oneFaceOf` for a front alone ([ADR 0106](adr/0106-store-each-card-s-template-and-each-block-s-version-and-id-derive-one-face-or-front-back-from-blanks-and-keep-unknown-shapes-unchanged.md));
  - `CardKey`, `describeContent`, the one line of text a card side shows, `spokenContent`, a side as a screen reader reads it, `CardSplitRow`, a card as a split row, and `CardBackRow`, a card's front with its back hidden or shown;
  - `WholeCardView`, which draws a whole card's front ([ADR 0068](adr/0068-store-a-table-or-cloze-card-as-typed-content-blocks-and-draw-it-whole-its-blanks-filled-in-place-once-answered.md)), and `playAudio`, which plays a sound block through `media` and says in a snack bar when it cannot, as study's actions and autoplay do;
  - `DeckRow`, a deck on a row, `DeckTile`, its image or else its icon on its colour, `showDeckAppearanceSheet`, the 덱 꾸미기 sheet, and `CardEditorScreen`, the card editor, with `deckStorageProvider` and `deckStorageKeys`, the device storage in which it keeps the label presets; and `PartOfSpeechPresetsRow`, the settings row that opens the "라벨" screen, which `home/` gives the account screen ([ADR 0100](adr/0100-merge-the-word-kind-into-basic-type-each-sense-s-label-and-offer-the-user-s-labels-above-the-keyboard.md));
  - the split card, drawn one way for every screen: `SplitCard`, given each side as a `SideBuilder` of the width its text is laid out in, which lays out a card's two halves divided by a line, side by side or, when a half would be too narrow at the text scale, stacked, each side centred or at the top left and, on the study card, scrolling inside its half once capped; and `ContentView`, which renders a side's content blocks, with an unsupported block as a labelled placeholder, fitting text through `CardText` in `app/lib/deck/src/card_text.dart`, or, given a height too, running the blocks together as one paragraph scaled down as far as the floor until it fits that height and cut there with an ellipsis. The study screen's cards, the card preview and `CardSplitRow` draw through them ([ADR 0035](adr/0035-show-the-previous-and-current-study-card-reach-earlier-cards-three-at-a-time-re-decide-the-centred-card-and-grade-each-card-by-its-colour-and-a-chip.md), [ADR 0042](adr/0042-show-only-the-previous-and-the-current-study-card-at-one-size-re-decide-the-previous-card-by-a-swipe-and-open-it-on-a-tap.md));

  - the batch save of incoming cards, from a photo or a copy: `AddTarget`, `AddToDeck`, `AddToNewDeck`, `IncomingCard`, `DuplicateChoice`, `CardsAdded`, `mergeBack`, and `AddSource` with `FromPhoto` and `FromCopy`;
  - `showNameSheet`, the sheet that asks for a deck's name, which `home/` and photo import use to name a deck;
  - the failures: `DeckFailure`, `StorageFailure`, `UnreadableCard`, `DeckDeleted`, `describeFailure`, the message a user reads for one, `describeProviderError`, the same for an error a provider holds, and `reportingFailure`, which shows an action's failure in a snack bar;

- The `study` domain reads a deck's cards through `DeckRepository.listCards`, oldest first.

### Model

- A deck has an id, a name, creation and update times, a visibility, an optional description, and an optional icon and colour, `DeckIcon` and `DeckColor`, each stored as a key; a null key, or one this build does not know, reads as none ([ADR 0103](adr/0103-give-each-deck-an-icon-and-a-colour-stored-as-two-unchecked-keys-and-drawn-on-its-tile-in-place-of-the-initial.md)). It also has an optional image, the `MediaId` of a file the `media` domain stores ([ADR 0107](adr/0107-store-media-in-a-private-supabase-storage-bucket-named-by-media-id-and-give-each-deck-an-image.md), [`media.md`](media.md)). The visibility is `PrivateDeck`, the default, or `PublicDeck` with its publish time ([`sharing.md`](sharing.md)).
- A card belongs to one deck and has a front, a back, a card key, a source, creation and update times, and the template it was made from with its version ([ADR 0106](adr/0106-store-each-card-s-template-and-each-block-s-version-and-id-derive-one-face-or-front-back-from-blanks-and-keep-unknown-shapes-unchanged.md)). Ids are UUIDs: new ones version 7, from the `uuid` package's `v7()`, which the composition site, `app/lib/main.dart`, passes to `DeckRepository` as `newId` ([ADR 0034](adr/0034-key-synced-rows-by-client-made-uuids-purge-tombstones-past-a-revision-horizon-and-index-each-query.md), [`sync.md`](sync.md)). Rows created before #124 (`2ad33491353dc8f4dfca12f906611a7c825ea3c0`, 2026-10-17), when it passed `v4()`, keep version 4 ids.
- `source` is `manual`, `import` or `copy`. The editor writes `manual`, photo import `import`, and a copy of another user's public deck `copy` ([ADR 0082](adr/0082-name-copying-a-public-deck-copy-and-keep-import-for-photo-import.md)), with the copied deck's id as `originDeckId`, null on every other card.
- Front and back are each an ordered list of content blocks. A block's kind is its type, `text`, `cloze`, `table`, `senses`, `image` or `audio`, and it has a version and an optional id; `UnsupportedContent` stands for a stored type, or a version, this build does not know ([ADR 0068](adr/0068-store-a-table-or-cloze-card-as-typed-content-blocks-and-draw-it-whole-its-blanks-filled-in-place-once-answered.md), [ADR 0106](adr/0106-store-each-card-s-template-and-each-block-s-version-and-id-derive-one-face-or-front-back-from-blanks-and-keep-unknown-shapes-unchanged.md)):
  - a `cloze` block holds its text, which writes each blank as `___`, and its answers, one non-empty answer per blank, in order;
  - a `table` block holds its column headers, its rows, each as many cells as there are columns and starting with the row's header, its blanks, each the row and column of a cell with text, distinct, and its spans, each a run of a body cell's text from a start to an end in UTF-16 code units, holding more than whitespace, overlapping no other span and in no cell that is a whole blank; it holds at least one blank or span ([ADR 0110](adr/0110-blank-a-span-of-a-table-cell-s-text-stored-at-table-block-v2-beside-whole-cell-blanks.md));
  - a `senses` block holds a vocabulary entry's senses, one or more, in the page's order, each a meaning, the labels the page prints for it in order and its examples; a label is a part of speech, one of fourteen with a fixed short form, or another label as printed ([ADR 0075](adr/0075-store-a-vocabulary-card-s-senses-as-a-typed-block-and-show-each-label-in-brackets-before-its-meaning.md));
  - an `image` block holds a photo by its media id, the stored image's width and height in pixels, and its boxes, each an id unique in the block, a left, top, width and height as fractions from 0 to 1 of the image's own width and height, never of a screen, inside the image, and an optional answer; fields this build does not know, on the payload or on a box, are written back as read ([ADR 0109](adr/0109-store-an-image-block-s-boxes-in-fractions-of-the-image-and-draw-them-with-the-image-s-one-transform.md));
  - an `audio` block holds a sound by its media id and its length in whole milliseconds above 0, `{media, duration_ms}`; fields this build does not know are written back as read; it is no blank ([ADR 0112](adr/0112-store-an-audio-block-by-media-id-and-length-up-to-60-seconds-and-play-the-front-s-sound-once-as-a-study-card-appears.md));
  - a block that breaks its type's shape is unreadable, as a text block without text is.
- A card is studied on one face when it holds a blank anywhere, a cloze or a table block or an image with a box, and front then back otherwise; `oneFaceOf` decides it from the blocks, the face being the front when it holds the blank, and otherwise the front's blocks followed by the back's, so the front stays the prompt, and nothing stores it ([ADR 0106](adr/0106-store-each-card-s-template-and-each-block-s-version-and-id-derive-one-face-or-front-back-from-blanks-and-keep-unknown-shapes-unchanged.md), [ADR 0108](adr/0108-add-a-free-template-and-an-add-control-that-puts-any-block-on-either-side-of-every-template.md)). The study views draw by it, never by the template.
- A whole card is a card whose front holds a cloze or a table block, or an image with a box: a table card's front is a text block with the table's name, then the table; a cloze card's front is its cloze. Its back is one text block, the front's text with its blanks filled, which photo import and a copy write. It is one card, with one key and one state. v6's cloze cards, a text front with `___`, stay split text cards.
- A senses block's text is one line per sense, each label in brackets and one space before the meaning, a part of speech by its short form: `[명] [사물] 기차`. Search, a screen reader, a merged back and the card key read it. Drawn, through `sensesSpan`, which `contentSpan` and `ContentView` use for every side, each label is its own span with a gap of `AppSpace.s4` after it, the closing bracket's letter spacing, before the meaning, and a sense with no label shows its meaning alone ([ADR 0075](adr/0075-store-a-vocabulary-card-s-senses-as-a-typed-block-and-show-each-label-in-brackets-before-its-meaning.md)). The study cards, the card preview, `CardSplitRow` and `CardBackRow` draw every side through them.
- `WholeCardView` draws a whole card's front from its blocks: a text block as its title; a cloze in its caller's style, centred, fitted to a width as `CardText` fits; a table in the body size, its header row and each row's first cell bold, its header row on `ground`, inside `outline` lines, a span drawn as a blank inside its cell's text; and, given a height, the whole front scaled down to fit it. An empty blank is its answer drawn in `hairline` on `hairline`, so filling it moves nothing; a filled one is the answer on `brandTint`. It reads as `spokenContent` says: each empty blank "빈칸", a span inside its cell's text, a table row by row, its cells joined by commas. Wherever a card is drawn split, a whole card is drawn whole through it: the study cards, `CardPreview` and `CardSplitRow` filled, and `CardBackRow` as below. `ContentView` draws a cloze or table block in a split side the same way, filled.
- An image block is drawn through `ImageBlockView` (`app/lib/deck/src/image_block.dart`) ([ADR 0109](adr/0109-store-an-image-block-s-boxes-in-fractions-of-the-image-and-draw-them-with-the-image-s-one-transform.md)):
  - The photo is drawn whole (`contain`), as large as fits the width and height its caller gives, through `media`'s `MediaImage`, on `ground` while it is read or cannot be. Its frame has the stored image's aspect ratio before the file is read.
  - Each box is placed in the same frame at its fractions of the frame's size, so the one transform that places the photo places the boxes, at every size and orientation.
  - A hidden box is filled in `hero`; a revealed one is outlined in `brand`, its answer, when it has one, on `brandTint` at its top left. The border, corners and label are in logical pixels and do not scale with the photo, which fits its space itself rather than being scaled as a picture.
  - `WholeCardView` draws it hidden or revealed as it draws a blank. `ContentView` draws it revealed in a split side, and a side that holds one is laid out block by block, scaled down as a whole to fit a height, in place of the one fitted paragraph.
  - It is read as "사진", then each box as "빈칸", or as its answer once revealed; `describeContent` gives "사진".
- An audio block is drawn through `AudioBlockView` (`app/lib/deck/src/audio_block.dart`) ([ADR 0112](adr/0112-store-an-audio-block-by-media-id-and-length-up-to-60-seconds-and-play-the-front-s-sound-once-as-a-study-card-appears.md)):
  - a round control 64 across on `brandTint`, a speaker in ink, named "소리 듣기", which plays the sound through `playAudio`, and its length under it, as minutes and seconds rounded up to the second;
  - `ContentView` and `WholeCardView` draw it centred among a side's blocks, and a side that holds one is laid out block by block, as a photo's is;
  - it is read, and `describeContent` gives it, as "소리".

### Storage

- The tables `decks` and `cards` are this domain's, held in the shared database `app/lib/database/database.dart`. The `reviews` and `card_states` tables there belong to the `study` domain ([`study.md`](study.md)).
- Every write sets the row's `updated_at` and leaves it unsynced (`dirty`), for sync to push ([`sync.md`](sync.md)).
- `decks` holds `visibility` (`private` or `public`), `published_at` and `description`; `cards` holds `origin_deck_id`. `setVisibility` writes the first three; a private deck keeps its description for the next publish. A deleted deck refuses it with `DeckDeleted`.
- A card side is stored as a JSON array of blocks, each `{type, v, id, payload}`: its kind, its version, its id and its payload. A new block type adds a discriminator value; stored rows need no migration, on the device or the server, whose columns hold any array ([ADR 0068](adr/0068-store-a-table-or-cloze-card-as-typed-content-blocks-and-draw-it-whole-its-blanks-filled-in-place-once-answered.md)).
- Versions and ids ([ADR 0106](adr/0106-store-each-card-s-template-and-each-block-s-version-and-id-derive-one-face-or-front-back-from-blanks-and-keep-unknown-shapes-unchanged.md)):
  - A write writes each block at the version this build writes: v2 for a table, v1 for every other type. A block stored without `v` is read as v1, and an older `v` is upgraded on read through `blockUpcasters`, one step per version: a v1 table becomes v2 with its blanks whole cells and no span ([ADR 0110](adr/0110-blank-a-span-of-a-table-cell-s-text-stored-at-table-block-v2-beside-whole-cell-blanks.md)).
  - A block gets an id from `DeckRepository`'s id generator on its first write, an existing block on its next. An edit keeps it: a rewritten block takes the id of the next block of its kind on that side. Ids are not compared, so two blocks of equal kind and content are equal.
  - A `v` that is not a positive integer, or an `id` that is not text, makes the side unreadable.
- A stored block of an unknown type, or of a known type at a version above this build's, is read as `UnsupportedContent` and written back byte for byte, so a card a later version wrote is shown, not lost.
- `cards` holds `template` and `template_version` since schema v11. Every write records `free` at version 1: `addCard`, `editCard`, a photo import, a copy and a merged back, but a merged back into a card this build cannot edit, which keeps its own ([ADR 0106](adr/0106-store-each-card-s-template-and-each-block-s-version-and-id-derive-one-face-or-front-back-from-blanks-and-keep-unknown-shapes-unchanged.md), [ADR 0114](adr/0114-write-every-card-as-a-free-block-list-remove-the-flip-cloze-and-table-forms-and-migrate-every-card-to-free.md)).
  - Schema v13 made every live card of `flip`, `cloze` or `table` at version 1, or of none, `free` at version 1, its sides, card key, `updated_at` and `dirty` kept; migration `20261027140000_card_template_free.sql` does the same on the server ([`sync.md`](sync.md), [`server.md`](server.md)). A tombstone, and a template or version no build of this app wrote, kept theirs.
- `decks` holds `image_media_id` since schema v12, null on a deck without an image. `setImage` writes it, unsynced, and releases the image it replaces through the `MediaStore` the repository is given, in the same transaction, unless another deck or card still holds it ([ADR 0107](adr/0107-store-media-in-a-private-supabase-storage-bucket-named-by-media-id-and-give-each-deck-an-image.md)).
- Deleting a deck sets `deleted_at` on it and on each of its cards, a tombstone sync pushes, and releases its image and its cards' photos and sounds. The UI asks for confirmation first and states how many cards go with it.
- Deleting a card sets its `deleted_at` and releases its photos and sounds. Its reviews and state stay in the database, out of every screen. The editor asks for confirmation first.
- An edit releases the photos and sounds the stored card held that its new sides do not, read from the stored row rather than the card the caller passes. Every release, of a deck's image or a card's photo or sound, happens after the write in its transaction, and only for a media id no other live card's side and no live deck's image on the device still holds, since a card copied within a deck keeps its source's media id, where a copy of a public deck, one's own included, stores its files under new ids ([ADR 0109](adr/0109-store-an-image-block-s-boxes-in-fractions-of-the-image-and-draw-them-with-the-image-s-one-transform.md), [ADR 0112](adr/0112-store-an-audio-block-by-media-id-and-length-up-to-60-seconds-and-play-the-front-s-sound-once-as-a-study-card-appears.md)).
- An edit that drops a photo the card held releases it, in the edit's transaction ([ADR 0109](adr/0109-store-an-image-block-s-boxes-in-fractions-of-the-image-and-draw-them-with-the-image-s-one-transform.md)). A side this build cannot decode releases none.
- Every read leaves tombstoned decks and cards out: the lists, the counts, `listCards`, and the duplicate check.
- Every write is local. The domain imports neither `sync` nor the network; a deck's image is read through `media`'s `MediaImage`, which downloads one the device does not hold.
- Stored vocabulary backs written as text with a printed label, such as `[명] 사과`, become senses blocks on the server (#228), by migration `20261019180003_senses_migration.sql` ([`server.md`](server.md), [ADR 0075](adr/0075-store-a-vocabulary-card-s-senses-as-a-typed-block-and-show-each-label-in-brackets-before-its-meaning.md)):
  - A card converts only when its back is one text block and every non-empty line is a sense line: a known short form, Korean or English (`명`, `동`, `n`, `adj` …), in brackets, in parentheses, with a dot, or bare and followed by a space and more text. Its back becomes one senses block, a sense per line, each labelled as the part of speech its short form names, with the label as printed.
  - A card with any other line is left alone, so a note or free text is never touched.
  - The rules are SQL functions the migration defines, `private.senses_block` and those it calls, and the dry run calls the same ones.
  - The dry run, `supabase/senses-dry-run.sh`, runs `supabase/senses-dry-run.sql` read-only against a running local stack, such as one holding a copy of production, and writes a report for the PM. The report counts the live cards, those that would convert and those left alone, by reason, and gives each form's cards, lines and up to five samples, every bare-form line, as its label may be a meaning's first word, and up to five mixed cards.
  - `supabase/senses-dry-run.test.sh`, in `make test`, checks it on a fixture of each form, a mixed card, a note, an unrecognised label, a back of two blocks and a deleted card, that no row changes, and that the migration then converts as many cards as the report counts.

### Card key and duplicates

- The card key is the front's text, a cloze's and a table's with their blanks filled, an image's media id then its answers, and a sound's media id, so two photo cards or two sound cards are never duplicates (`plainText`), normalized in this order: Unicode NFKC, trim, collapse each whitespace run to one space, case-fold. Punctuation and accents are kept.
- Case-folding is Dart's lowercase mapping, which matches Unicode simple case folding; full folding such as `ß` to `ss` is not applied.
- A duplicate is another card in the same deck with an equal key. Cards in other decks are never duplicates.
- Adding or editing a card whose key collides is refused with the existing card, unless the caller allows the duplicate. The editor then opens a bottom sheet, "이 덱에 이미 있는 카드예요", which says that case and spacing are ignored, shows the existing card as a split card, and offers "기존 카드 열기", which opens the existing card over the editor so that closing it returns to the card being typed (issue #309), and, last, "그래도 추가" (or "그래도 저장" when editing); dismissing it does neither ([ADR 0024](adr/0024-one-voice-and-a-sheet-action-rule.md)). No card is overwritten or deleted by the rule.
- The check is one lookup on the `(deck_id, card_key)` index, inside the same transaction as the write.

### Import

- `DeckRepository.addCards` saves a list of `IncomingCard`s into an existing deck, or into a new deck it creates, in one transaction: a failure writes nothing, the new deck included.
- Each card's sides are content blocks, a table or cloze card's front kept as its blocks, and it is written unsynced like every write, with the source its `AddSource` gives: `import` from a photo, or `copy` and the origin deck from a public deck ([`sharing.md`](sharing.md)).
- A copy's photos and sounds become the user's own ([ADR 0113](adr/0113-copy-a-public-deck-s-photos-and-sounds-into-the-copier-s-own-media-under-new-ids-when-the-deck-is-copied.md)):
  - before the transaction, `addCards` reads each file the cards name from the folder of the owner `FromCopy` names, through `MediaStore.fetchShared`, since a network request inside the transaction would hold every other write. Each file is kept on the device under a new media id as it arrives, and only its id and metadata are held;
  - inside it, `MediaStore.addFetched` queues each file for upload as the card holding it is written, and the block names its id;
  - a front is rewritten before its key is taken, and a back only when its card is added, so a skipped or merged card queues none of its back's files;
  - after the transaction, `MediaStore.discardFetched` deletes from the device each fetched file no written card holds, and every one when the transaction fails;
  - a file several cards hold is stored once, and one that could not be read leaves its block naming a new id no file holds.
  - Every copy makes new ids, and a photo's or a sound's key is its media id, so a re-copy into a deck finds no photo or sound card a duplicate and adds it whatever the choice.
- An import into a deck deleted meanwhile, on this device or through sync, is refused with `DeckDeleted` and writes nothing.
- A card whose key collides with a card in the deck, or with one written earlier in the same import, does what its `DuplicateChoice` says, as the PM decided for issue #30:
  - **skip** (건너뛰기) writes nothing;
  - **merge back** (뒷면 합치기) appends the card's back to the existing card's back as a new text block, keeping its id, front and reviews; when a line of that back already equals it under the card key normalization, nothing changes;
  - **add as new** (새 카드로 추가) adds the card beside the existing one.
- No card is overwritten or deleted. The choice is per card; photo import gives every card it writes merge back, so a card whose key the deck holds, or one written earlier in the same import, is merged rather than doubled. It imports only the cards of the rows its list includes, each in its section's form, so a row left out is neither written nor merged ([`ai-extraction.md`](ai-extraction.md)).

### Failures

- Each expected failure has a type:
  - a colliding key is the save result `DuplicateFront`;
  - a block of an unknown type is `UnsupportedContent`;
  - a database exception is `StorageFailure`;
  - stored content that cannot be decoded is `UnreadableCard`. `addCard` and `editCard` read the row they wrote back inside their transaction, so content the decoder refuses fails the save as `UnreadableCard` and is never stored (issue #306);
  - a write into a deck that was deleted is `DeckDeleted`.
- `StorageFailure`, `UnreadableCard` and `DeckDeleted` are `DeckFailure`s the repository throws, or emits on a watched stream.
- The screens show a `DeckFailure` to the user as a localized message, in a snack bar for an action or in place of a list. Any other error propagates.

### Interface

- Every string the screens show comes from the shared ARB set.
- Every tap target carries a semantic label: its text, or a tooltip for an icon button.
- The screens follow the principles and the theme of [`design.md`](design.md). Widget tests check each screen against Flutter's `labeledTapTargetGuideline`, `androidTapTargetGuideline` and `textContrastGuideline` in the light and the dark theme.
- An empty deck name or card side is refused with a message on its field.

### Row

- `DeckRow` draws a deck on a rounded surface: its 44 px tile (`DeckTile`), hidden from screen readers beside the name; its name, a "공개" chip when it is public, and what its caller shows of today; and under them what its caller shows of its progress, or "카드 n장" without it. A tap opens the deck.
- `DeckTile` draws the deck's image, cropped square to the tile's rounded corners, when it has one; while the image is read, and when it cannot be, it draws the icon ([ADR 0107](adr/0107-store-media-in-a-private-supabase-storage-bucket-named-by-media-id-and-give-each-deck-an-image.md)). It draws the deck's icon in ink, at half the tile's side, on its colour from the theme's deck palette. ### State and composition

- State and injection use Riverpod. `deckRepositoryProvider` has no implementation of its own; the composition site, `app/lib/main.dart`, overrides it with the repository it constructs.
- The domain imports `media`, through its surface, for a deck's image and a card's photos and sounds, and nothing else. The home and deck screens that show its decks beside other domains' widgets are `home/`'s ([`home.md`](home.md), [ADR 0039](adr/0039-name-every-top-level-folder-by-its-responsibility-compose-across-domains-in-a-home-shell-and-keep-seams-off-screens.md)).
- The repository receives its clock and id generator, so tests fix both, and the `MediaStore` that releases a let-go image; a repository given none, as in a test that stores no image, releases none. It also streams each deck's card count, which the 덱 tab shows.
