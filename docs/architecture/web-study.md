# Web study

The web's `study` feature: the 오늘 page, the study page and its session, the 기록 page, the study day and its time zone, the queue and today's list, the engine in the browser and on the server, and the answers' writes. The pages' shell and the server reads they share are [`web.md`](web.md)'s; the app's `study` domain is [`study.md`](study.md).

## Current decisions

### Reads

- `/` (오늘) reads `decks`' `readDeckList` (every deck, and each card's id, deck and creation time, without its sides), `study`'s `readToday` with the streak and `study`'s `readRecentRetention`, the server's `study_record_days` alone, with no engine, kept to the last 30 study days ([ADR 0047](adr/0047-read-the-web-record-through-two-server-functions-and-fold-it-through-the-engine-on-the-server.md)), together.
- `/record` (기록) reads `study`'s `readRecord`: the server's `study_record_days` and `study_record_cards` in two requests sent together, each row parsed at the boundary, in the day of the cookie's zone, and the cards' answers folded through the engine on the server, where a state or an answer the engine refuses reads as `unreadable` ([`server.md`](server.md), [ADR 0047](adr/0047-read-the-web-record-through-two-server-functions-and-fold-it-through-the-engine-on-the-server.md)).
- `/study` reads `readLibrary` over the decks it names (those decks and their cards with both sides) and `readToday` with the streak, together. When a card holds a sound, it then signs every sound of the decks' cards in one Storage request, `study`'s `signSounds`, each at `{user}/{media id}` for 4 hours; a sound Storage signs none for, and every sound when the request fails, has no address ([ADR 0112](adr/0112-store-an-audio-block-by-media-id-and-length-up-to-60-seconds-and-play-the-front-s-sound-once-as-a-study-card-appears.md)).
- `readToday` reads today's study day ([ADR 0033](adr/0033-count-cards-left-per-study-day-from-04-00-and-continue-as-unrecorded-practice.md), [ADR 0043](adr/0043-set-each-day-s-new-cards-from-the-user-s-recency-weighted-study-time-and-pace-and-count-an-answer-s-time-only-in-the-foreground.md)): every answer since the day began, then, for the cards answered today, their answers before it, in requests of 100 card ids each, sent together, to count the new cards first answered today and the reviews the day held when it started; the stored state of every reviewed card, across every deck (`readCardStates`); and the server's `study_days_before` over the latest 30 study days before today in one request, each answer counted up to 30 s, from which `web/src/features/study/study-time.ts` computes the user's pace as the phone does; the user's own `study_settings` row, the daily study minutes, or 20 without one ([ADR 0085](adr/0085-fill-each-day-s-new-cards-from-the-daily-study-minutes-the-user-sets-stored-per-user-on-the-server-and-synced.md)); from the minutes, the reviews due as the day stood when it started and the pace, `study-time.ts` computes the day's new cards, and `readToday` returns the minutes and those reviews beside the day; and, for the streak, the server's `study_streak_before` over the days before today in one request, in one request ([`server.md`](server.md)). It reads the day in the time zone the cookie `smartman-tz` names, which the root layout's head script writes on every page from the browser's `Intl` zone, the sign-in page included, so it is set before any page that counts; without the cookie, or with a zone the server does not know, it reads the day in Asia/Seoul (`web/src/features/study/time-zone.ts`).

### Queue and today's list

- `web/src/features/study/queue.ts` holds the queue rules of [`study.md`](study.md), in one module: the study day from 04:00 to 04:00 in a time zone, with its new cards less those first answered in it across every deck, never below zero and not carried over, and the constants of a day's new cards, which `study-time.ts` applies; a card due when it falls due before the day ends; a backlog above three days of reviews due, its due cards earliest due first in shuffled runs of ten and no new card; otherwise the due cards and as many new ones as the day has left, oldest first, shuffled; a "모른다" shown again after 3 to 5 other cards; the streak; and the counts the deck screens show, from the same selection ([ADR 0025](adr/0025-order-a-review-backlog-by-due-date-and-pause-new-cards.md), [ADR 0033](adr/0033-count-cards-left-per-study-day-from-04-00-and-continue-as-unrecorded-practice.md)). The study page selects the queue on the server, in the day `readToday` read, and the browser orders it from a random source seeded with that time, so the server's render and the browser's agree. It also gives each card each side's one line of text, which `decks` describes, since the study screen is a client module and cannot import `decks`' entry point ([`structure.md`](structure.md)). Every random choice of a session, its order, each re-show gap and each answer's fuzz factor, comes from that one source.
- `web/src/features/study/today-list.ts` places cards in today's list through the WebAssembly's `todaysList` ([ADR 0061](adr/0061-keep-a-card-failed-today-in-today-s-list-until-it-is-passed-through-a-pure-engine-function-over-the-day-s-reviews.md)), and throws where the list does not place every card given. `dayPlanOf` makes the day plan from it, as the phone's does: the selection the rules take, the cards still on "모른다" that they did not, and the cards done ([ADR 0062](adr/0062-take-the-study-queue-and-the-top-bar-s-count-from-today-s-list-so-a-reopened-day-goes-on-in-the-app-and-the-web.md)).
  - The 오늘 page's count, bar and deck rows, the deck page's count and the deck list's chips all count the plan's left cards, `countDay` and `dailyGoal`, so they agree with the study page and the phone. Each page loads the server's engine module first (`readyServerEngine`) and shows its own failure message if it cannot.
  - The study page's server sends the selection, the cards still on "모른다" apart from it, whether a card of the day is done, the day's bounds, today's answers with their cards and times, and the states of the cards answered today. The browser's session puts each card still on "모른다" back after a re-show's gap or at the end, counts its own answers in the day it opened in, after 04:00 too, and counts the day as the phone's does.
  - The screen starts the session once the engine's module has loaded in the browser, showing the page's skeleton until then. If the module fails to load, it says "학습 화면을 불러오지 못했어요. 연결을 확인하고 다시 시도해 주세요." with "다시 시도", which loads it again, and "그만하기", a link to where the session was opened from.
  - When the decks cannot be read, the page says why, as the other pages do, with "그만하기", a link to where the session was opened from: the study page stands outside the shell, so no menu leads back.
- The day plan (`dayPlanOf`, `today-list.ts`) leaves out the cards studied past it, `studyDay`'s `extra`, as the phone's does: neither selected, left nor done ([ADR 0087](adr/0087-count-the-cards-studied-past-the-day-s-plan-apart-so-they-never-reopen-the-day-or-grow-its-goal.md)).
- `web/src/features/study/overview.ts` copies `overview.dart`'s definitions: progress at 21 days, a deck's counts, the next review, the last seven study days, the catch-up, the daily goal and today's status, with `chartDays` and `masteredInterval` equal to `overview.dart`'s. Its `lastWeek` takes each day's cards from `study_record_days`, which 오늘's retention card reads, each earlier day's time from `study_days_before`, which the pace reads (`readToday`'s `earlierTimes`), and today's from today's answers, so 오늘 makes no new read.
- Each queue constant in `queue.ts` equals `app/lib/study/src/queue.dart`'s of the same name, and `studyDayStartHour` equals the hour `public.study_day_start_hour()` returns in the latest migration defining it ([`server.md`](server.md)).
- `web/src/features/study/stats.ts` and `study-time.ts` give the figures of the phone's `app/test/study/stats_test.dart` and `study_time_test.dart` on the same fixed histories.

  - once no card is left, the completion card in the current card's place, as on the phone: the mascot in its 84 px circle, "오늘 학습 완료", today's "n 안다", "n 모른다", minutes and streak after a flame in the `streak` colour over the decks, "학습하기" while new cards are left past today's, or "더 학습할 카드가 없어요" where none is, and "그만하기", a 44 px link to where the session was opened from; the first of these choices takes the focus. "학습하기" goes on with the new cards past today's on the same page, as the phone's does, each answer written and counted apart from the day's plan; the study page passes them in its plan, oldest first, with the cards already answered past the plan today. A page opened with no card left today starts on them at once. With no card left to take and nothing answered today the page says "오늘은 학습할 카드가 없어요", with no progress bar ([ADR 0049](adr/0049-study-in-one-flow-that-goes-on-past-today-s-cards-as-recorded-study-with-the-current-card-showing-its-front-alone.md)).
  - The close button is a link named "그만하기" to where the session was opened from, as the completion card's is, and as the phone's close returns to the screen that opened study (#210, #327).
- An answer's duration is the time its card was on screen with the page visible: the screen pauses the session's clock while `document.visibilityState` is `hidden` and starts it again on `visibilitychange` back to visible; an answer given while hidden counts up to hiding. The window's focus pauses nothing ([ADR 0043](adr/0043-set-each-day-s-new-cards-from-the-user-s-recency-weighted-study-time-and-pace-and-count-an-answer-s-time-only-in-the-foreground.md)).
- Keyboard: → is "안다" and ← is "모른다" for the current card, and J and K answer nothing (#273); Z toggles the last answer, Esc follows "그만하기" to where the session was opened from or closes the card's dialog, and ? lists these in a dialog, closed by "닫기", Esc or a click outside it; ↑, ↓ and the wheel do nothing. Z is read by its physical key, so it works in any input language. Each has a visible button carrying it in `aria-keyshortcuts`.

### Engine and writes

- The engine runs in the browser ([ADR 0022](adr/0022-study-on-the-web-with-the-engine-in-the-browser.md)). `web/src/features/study/engine-wasm` links to `engine-wasm/pkg/`, which `next.config.ts` admits in `turbopackAdditionalRoots`; the generated glue fetches its `.wasm`, which the build serves as a static file. The screen starts fetching it when it opens. The `/record` page also runs it on the server: `web/src/features/study/engine-server.ts` reads the same module's file from the project's root, through that link, and instantiates it once per server process, for each card's recall and the states its answers fold into ([ADR 0047](adr/0047-read-the-web-record-through-two-server-functions-and-fold-it-through-the-engine-on-the-server.md)).
- Writes never hold the screen: an answer or a toggle changes it at once, and its write joins a queue that sends one write at a time, in order. A write replays the card's answers of this session, with their fuzz factors, from its state when the session opened, as the phone does, and the Server Action `recordAnswer` stores the result with the session it verifies ([`sync.md`](sync.md)). Each new review's id is a version 7 uuid from the `uuid` package's `v7()` ([ADR 0034](adr/0034-key-synced-rows-by-client-made-uuids-purge-tombstones-past-a-revision-horizon-and-index-each-query.md) §1).
- A write that fails stops the queue: it and every write behind it wait, so a toggle never reaches the server before its answer. The screen says what failed in one sentence with "다시 시도", which sends them again; the answers stay in the stack. While a write waits, unloading the page asks first, through the browser's `beforeunload`. Leaving by the page's own links, the close, Esc and the completion card's "그만하기", sends the waiting writes first, the failed one again, and then goes. If one fails again, a dialog, "저장하지 못한 응답이 있어요", says in one sentence that leaving drops them, with "취소" focused and, last, "나가기" as a red final button; "취소", Esc or a click outside stays (#323).
- `recordAnswer` reads the card id through `decks`' `parseCardId` and refuses one that is not a uuid, writing nothing.
- The account page's "공부" row (`study`'s `DailyMinutes`, `web/src/features/study/components/daily-minutes.tsx`, [`web-account.md`](web-account.md)) opens a dialog, "하루 공부 시간", with the app's picker's content ([ADR 0086](adr/0086-show-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-on-the-today-card-the-completion-card-a-one-time-first-run-step-the-account-screen-and-the-web-s-today-page.md), [ADR 0090](adr/0090-pick-the-daily-study-minutes-as-one-large-number-between-minus-and-plus-typed-in-place-with-two-tiles-of-what-they-mean.md), [ADR 0091](adr/0091-draw-the-today-card-as-a-progress-bar-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-only-in-settings.md)): the minutes as one large number, a button read as "하루 n분, 눌러서 직접 입력", between "5분 줄이기" and "5분 늘리기", which move them by `dailyMinuteStep` within the bounds and disable at each, handing the focus to the other; the number pressed turns into a text input with `inputmode="numeric"`, committed on Enter or blur, with "5분에서 180분 사이로 적어 주세요" and "저장" disabled outside the bounds, and an empty input keeping the minutes; the two tiles as a polite `status` from `minutesPreview` (`study-time.ts`) over the reviews due at the day's start, the cards started today and those never answered, following the input as it is typed, in a row of one height whether the number or the input is shown; and "저장". Focus starts on the number, returns to it after Enter and to the row on close, and Tab stays inside the dialog; Escape, the close button or a click outside closes it without saving. "저장" calls `saveDailyMinutes`, shows its refusal in the dialog, and otherwise closes it and reads the page again.
- The Server Action `saveDailyMinutes` (`web/src/features/study/save-daily-minutes.ts`, through `study`'s `actions.ts`) saves the signed-in user's daily study minutes through the server's `save_study_settings`, set at the time of the save, so the later edit wins against the phone's, and revalidates the pages that count today ([ADR 0085](adr/0085-fill-each-day-s-new-cards-from-the-daily-study-minutes-the-user-sets-stored-per-user-on-the-server-and-synced.md), [`server.md`](server.md)). Minutes outside 5 to 180, or not whole, are refused unsent.
