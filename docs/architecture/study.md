# Study

The `study` domain: studying one or more decks, scheduling their cards for review, and the bridge through which it calls the Rust engine.

## Current decisions

### Surface and location

- The domain lives in `app/lib/study/`; its public surface is `app/lib/study/study.dart`, with internals under `app/lib/study/src/` ([ADR 0002](adr/0002-domain-public-surface-and-shared-database.md)).
- The surface exports what the composition site constructs and overrides: `StudyRepository`, `Scheduler`, `localOffsetAt`, and the providers `studyRepositoryProvider`, `schedulerProvider`, `studyClockProvider`, `studyZoneProvider` and `studyRandomProvider`; and what `home/` composes: `StudyScreen`, `TodayCounter`, and the count it shows, `TodayCount` with `TodayPending`, `TodayCounted`, which carries the time one card takes and the minutes the session takes, and `TodayUnavailable`, and `TodayBuilder`; `TodayOverviewReader` and what it reads, `TodayOverviewRead` with `OverviewPending`, `OverviewUnavailable` and `TodayOverview`, with `DailyGoal`; and `ProgressCounter` with `DeckProgress` and `ProgressBuilder` ([`home.md`](home.md)).
- `Scheduler.fold` is the fold `sync` recomputes a card's state with: the card's stored reviews in `reviewed_at` order, through the engine from a new card, each with its stored fuzz factor and a null factor as none ([`sync.md`](sync.md)). The domain does not import `sync`.
- The domain reads the decks' cards only through `deck`'s surface (`DeckRepository.listCards`, [`deck.md`](deck.md)).

### Session

- A session studies a `DeckSelection`: one deck from its deck screen or its row on the 오늘 tab, the selected decks from the 덱 tab's selection mode, or every deck from the 오늘 tab's today, backlog or done card. `home/` opens `StudyScreen` and builds `TodayCounter` itself ([ADR 0009](adr/0009-multi-deck-session-and-random-queue-order.md), [ADR 0039](adr/0039-name-every-top-level-folder-by-its-responsibility-compose-across-domains-in-a-home-shell-and-keep-seams-off-screens.md)).
- A study day runs from 04:00 (`studyDayStartHour`) to the next 04:00 in the device's time zone, each boundary read at the instant it names, so a day across a change of offset still starts and ends at 04:00 local time. `studyZoneProvider` gives the zone, as the offset from UTC at an instant; the composition site passes `localOffsetAt`, and tests a fixed zone ([ADR 0033](adr/0033-count-cards-left-per-study-day-from-04-00-and-continue-as-unrecorded-practice.md)).
- A day takes `newCardLimit` (20) new cards less the cards first answered in it, across every deck, from the stored reviews, never below zero; an unused quota is not carried to the next day.
- The queue is built when the session opens, from the cards of every selected deck: the cards left today. A card is due when it falls due before the day ends ([ADR 0025](adr/0025-order-a-review-backlog-by-due-date-and-pause-new-cards.md), [ADR 0033](adr/0033-count-cards-left-per-study-day-from-04-00-and-continue-as-unrecorded-practice.md)):
  - the daily load is the sum, over every reviewed card of the decks, of one over its interval in days (due time less last review), at least one;
  - the decks are in backlog when their due cards outnumber `backlogDays` (3) times the daily load;
  - in backlog, the queue is every due card, earliest due first, equal due times in the order read, and no new card; each run of `backlogBucketSize` (10) consecutive cards is shuffled in its place;
  - otherwise, the queue is every due card and as many cards never reviewed across the decks as the day has left, oldest first by creation time, shuffled together, so each new card lands at a random place among the due ones ([ADR 0009](adr/0009-multi-deck-session-and-random-queue-order.md)).
- Each shuffle is a forward Fisher–Yates over its run, the whole queue out of backlog, a draw of 0 keeping the card in its place.
- A "Don't know" puts the card back after a gap drawn from `reshowGapMin` (3) to `reshowGapMax` (5) other cards, or after as many as remain. Each re-show is a review passed to the engine.
- `newCardLimit`, `studyDayStartHour`, `reshowGapMin`, `reshowGapMax`, `backlogDays` and `backlogBucketSize` are constants in `app/lib/study/src/queue.dart`. The web app applies the same rules, with the same constants, in `web/src/features/study/queue.ts` ([`web.md`](web.md)); a change to them changes both, and `web/src/features/study/__tests__/rules.test.ts` fails when one differs from the other's.
- Every random choice of a session comes from one `Random`, which `studyRandomProvider` makes from the session's start time. The composition site seeds it from that time; tests inject their own.
- `TodayCounter` counts the cards left today, the queue a session over its decks would build now, as due cards and new ones, or as due cards in a backlog, and passes the count to the builder `home/` gives it: a deck row's chip, a row of the 오늘 tab's decks, and the deck screen's navy card. In a backlog the deck screen adds "밀린 복습을 마칠 때까지 새 카드는 쉬어요.", and the 오늘 tab shows its backlog card ([`home.md`](home.md)). `readToday` reads the cards, their states, the study day, the queue and the count once, for `TodayCounter` and `TodayOverviewReader` alike. It reads the count when it is built, when its decks change, and when its route comes back on top; a failed read is passed on as its localized message.
- `TodayOverviewReader` reads, for every deck, what the 오늘 tab shows ([ADR 0036](adr/0036-organise-the-app-in-today-decks-and-record-tabs-and-define-the-daily-goal-forecast-and-card-progress.md)), each from `app/lib/study/src/overview.dart` or the queue's own rules:
  - the count, as `TodayCounter` reads it;
  - the daily goal (`DailyGoal`): every card of the selection answered within the study day, from the stored reviews, together with every card left in today's queue, each once; done is the goal less the cards left, so a card answered "Don't know" and due again today is not done. Practice records nothing and counts toward neither;
  - the streak, `streak` over the answers since the day began and `studyDaysBefore`, as the completion card counts it;
  - the forecast (`forecast`): today's count, then, for each of the next six study days (`forecastDays` is 7), the stored states that fall due within it, each day from 04:00 to 04:00 in the device's zone, with the calendar date each starts on;
  - the next review: the first later day of the forecast with a card due;
  - in a backlog, the catch-up (`catchUp`): the overdue reviews left at the start of each of the seven days when the user clears the average daily load a day, `dailyLoad` over the decks' stored states rounded up and at least one.
- A card is mastered (익힘) when its interval, its due time less its last review, is at least `masteredInterval` (21 days), learning (학습 중) when reviewed below it, and new (새 카드) without a stored state (`progressOf`). `ProgressCounter` counts a selection's cards by progress (`DeckProgress`) for the deck rows and the deck screen. Both counters read again when their decks change and when their route comes back on top.
- The count carries the time one card takes the user: the median `duration_ms` of their latest 200 reviews that are not deleted, or 10 s while they have fewer than 20, which the today card turns into minutes ([ADR 0027](adr/0027-draw-both-clients-in-the-smartman-brand-and-the-v2-screens.md)).
- The screen has a top bar (the close button, an 8 px rounded progress bar in the ink colour and the count of cards answered out of the session's cards, "15/20" at 14/700) above one scroll view: every card answered so far, then the current card, or the completion card once no card is left ([ADR 0007](adr/0007-study-swipe-screen-and-session-toggle.md), [ADR 0023](adr/0023-scroll-the-current-study-card-with-the-stack-and-centre-each-half.md), [ADR 0035](adr/0035-show-the-previous-and-current-study-card-reach-earlier-cards-three-at-a-time-re-decide-the-centred-card-and-grade-each-card-by-its-colour-and-a-chip.md)). It shows no deck name and no hint text, and never builds the next card.
- Under the current card, "모른다" after a left chevron and "안다" before a right chevron are 44 px pill buttons on the red and green tints, at 14/700, which answer it as a swipe does, each with Material's padded tap target ([ADR 0035](adr/0035-show-the-previous-and-current-study-card-reach-earlier-cards-three-at-a-time-re-decide-the-centred-card-and-grade-each-card-by-its-colour-and-a-chip.md)).
- The current card:
  - sits full width less 20 on each side, last in the scroll view and painted over the stack, its centre at 57% of the screen height while it and the answer buttons fit above the bottom gap when the view is at its end;
  - is rounded 24 with a two-layer soft shadow, and its halves are divided by a line that stops 28 short of the card's edges;
  - holds the question on its left half and the answer on its right, hidden behind an eye in the muted colour until a tap and hidden again by the next; when a half would be narrower than 140 at the current text scale, the answer sits under the question, in two halves of the same height divided at the middle;
  - centres each half's content across and down while it fits, and shrinks the text, the question from 30 to 18 and the answer from 20 to 15, until its widest word fits the half, so a word breaks inside itself only past that floor (`CardText`, [`design.md`](design.md));
  - draws its halves through `deck`'s `SplitCard` and each side's content blocks through `deck`'s `ContentView`, the question at 30/800 and the answer at 20/600, with `UnsupportedContent` as a labelled placeholder ([`deck.md`](deck.md));
  - grows with its content from 200 to 55% of the screen height, then each half scrolls inside, from the top.
- A horizontal drag of the current card moves and tilts it; past 40 a chip and a tint name the answer. Released past 100, or flung faster than 700 per second, it answers "Know" to the right and "Don't know" to the left, with a light haptic; otherwise it springs back. A vertical drag scrolls the view.
- The previous cards are every answer of the session, re-shows included, newest nearest the current card, each in a slot 36% of the view's height, built only near the view. Each is 120 high, full width less 24 on each side, rounded 20, and shows its grade ([ADR 0035](adr/0035-show-the-previous-and-current-study-card-reach-earlier-cards-three-at-a-time-re-decide-the-centred-card-and-grade-each-card-by-its-colour-and-a-chip.md)):
  - "안다" on the `knowCard` fill inside a 1.5 px `knowLine`, "모른다" on `dontKnowCard` inside `dontKnowLine`, with no shadow;
  - a chip on its top edge, a check or a cross and the word at 12/700, white on the grade's colour, so the grade never rests on colour alone;
  - two lines of each side, the front at 18/700 and the back at 16 in `ink2`, centred in its half and shrunk to 12 until its widest word fits;
  - a long-press that opens the whole card in a sheet.
- The scroll view ([ADR 0023](adr/0023-scroll-the-current-study-card-with-the-stack-and-centre-each-half.md), [ADR 0035](adr/0035-show-the-previous-and-current-study-card-reach-earlier-cards-three-at-a-time-re-decide-the-centred-card-and-grade-each-card-by-its-colour-and-a-chip.md)):

  - dragged down, reveals earlier cards, the current card moving down with them, and released comes to rest with a previous card at its centre, or at its end;
  - scrolled back, paints the card at its centre at full size and opacity, and every other card, the current card included, at 90% of its size and 72% opacity, from each card's distance to the centre; a fade under the top bar hides the cards scrolled beneath it, and "지금 카드로", a primary button over a fade at the bottom, returns to the end, at once under reduce motion;

The days before the one the session opened in are those the walk above counted; that day and any after it are read from the answers stored since it began and the session's own.
  - The stack's answers can still be changed; a change that puts a card back takes the completion card away until the card is answered.
- "계속 학습" goes on as practice in the same screen, with nothing that marks it apart: every card of the decks answered today, before or in this session, shuffled; then the cards missed in that pass, each put back after the re-show gap until it is answered right; then all again, until the user leaves. Its answers join the stack, and a change of a card's last answer in the pass under way has the same effects on the practice queue as on the session's. Practice writes no review, no card state and nothing for sync, and changes no count; the top bar stays as the session left it. A change of one of the session's own answers during practice is still written, without changing the practice queue.

Its colours are the app theme's tokens, in the light or the dark theme, and its text styles come from the app theme ([`design.md`](design.md), [ADR 0011](adr/0011-shared-app-theme-bundled-font-and-today-builder.md)).

### Storage

- The `reviews` and `card_states` tables in `app/lib/database/database.dart` belong to this domain. `card_states` holds each reviewed card's stability, difficulty, last review and due time; a new card has no row. Schema v2 added it ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-study-queue-and-card-state.md)).
- Every answer of the day's cards writes one `reviews` row (`good` for "Know", `again` for "Don't know", with its time, duration on screen and fuzz factor, which the answer draws from the session's `Random`). Schema v3 added `fuzz_factor`, checked to `[0, 1)`; a row written before it holds null, as its review was not fuzzed ([ADR 0012](adr/0012-fuzz-review-intervals.md)).
- Each write, after an answer or a toggle, replays the card's answers in this session as they stand, from its state when the session opened, one engine review per answer with that answer's fuzz factor. It then rewrites those `reviews` rows, keeping their ids, times and factors, and the card's `card_states` row, in one transaction ([ADR 0007](adr/0007-study-swipe-screen-and-session-toggle.md)). Each row it writes takes the write's time as `updated_at` and is left unsynced; sync pushes it, the server keeping a review's rating by the later `updated_at` ([`sync.md`](sync.md)).
- Schema v4 added `updated_at`, `deleted_at` and `dirty` to both tables. Reads leave out a card state with `deleted_at` set.
- The engine calls and the write run after the screen has changed, chained in the order given.
- `StudyRepository` receives its id generator, and the session its clock (`studyClockProvider`), zone (`studyZoneProvider`) and random source (`studyRandomProvider`), so tests fix all four.
- When a session opens it also reads, through `StudyRepository`, for the quota, the completion line and the streak; deleted reviews are left out, and a review counts whether or not its card is live ([`sync.md`](sync.md)):
  - the first answer of each card first answered since the day began, grouping only the cards answered since then;
  - every answer since then;
  - for each earlier study day, back from the day before, the latest answer before the day ends, one probe of `reviews_live_reviewed_at` per day, stopping at the first day without one ([ADR 0034](adr/0034-key-synced-rows-by-client-made-uuids-purge-tombstones-past-a-revision-horizon-and-index-each-query.md) §4).
  - Each of these reads is SQL comparing the stored text, so it reaches its index; drift's own comparison of times would go through `julianday()`.

### The bridge to the engine

- The app calls the engine ([`engine.md`](engine.md)) through flutter_rust_bridge 2.13.0, built with its native-assets backend ([ADR 0004](adr/0004-flutter-rust-bridge.md)).
- The bridge crate is `rust_lib_smartman` in `app/rust/`. It depends on `engine/` by path and declares the Dart surface in `app/rust/src/api/engine.rs`:
  - `review_new_card` — a new card's first review, with an optional fuzz factor;
  - `review_card` — a review of a card from its persisted state parts, with an optional fuzz factor;
  - `replay` — a history folded into a state;
  - `retrievability` — the probability of recall at a time.
- Times cross the bridge as whole seconds since the Unix epoch, as the engine holds them. Answers cross as the app's two buttons, `Answer.know` and `Answer.dontKnow`.
- The generated Dart bindings are in `app/lib/study/src/rust/`; only this domain imports them.
- `Scheduler.load()` runs `RustLib.init()`, which loads the library, and returns the scheduler that calls it. The composition site awaits it before `runApp`.

### Failures

- Every engine `Error` variant arrives in Dart as one variant of the sealed `EngineError`, thrown by the call that was refused. `EngineError.unknownRating` cannot arise from this surface, which takes answers, not rating numbers.
- The bridge code itself does not panic: `app/rust/src/api/engine.rs` denies `panic!`, `unwrap`, `expect` and indexing, and `make lint` runs clippy over it. A panic inside flutter_rust_bridge's generated code reaches Dart as its `PanicException`, as an unexpected failure.
- The domain's expected failures are the sealed `StudyFailure`:
  - `SchedulingFailure` wraps an `EngineError`, thrown by `Scheduler`;
  - `AnswerNotSaved` is a failed write, and `StudyStorageFailure` a failed read, thrown by `StudyRepository`.
- A failure of a write reaches one handler, which the study screen registers with the session: a localized snack bar on the app's messenger, shown even if the screen has closed. Any other error a write raises goes to `FlutterError.reportError`, and the writes queued after it still run. A failure reading the queue is shown in place of the session; any other error there propagates.

### Build and generated code

- `flutter build` builds the crate through the hook in `app/hook/build.dart`, with the Rust release pinned in `app/rust/rust-toolchain.toml`, for every target Flutter asks for. No Android or iOS build file names the crate.
- `app/flutter_rust_bridge.yaml` configures the codegen. Its output is committed: `app/rust/src/frb_generated.rs` and `app/lib/study/src/rust/`, including the `freezed` part file of `EngineError`. `check-generated.sh` reruns the codegen, at the release `app/rust/Cargo.lock` pins, and fails when the output differs.
- To change the surface: edit `app/rust/src/api/`, run `flutter_rust_bridge_codegen generate` in `app/`, and commit what it writes.

- `flutter test` loads the library from `app/rust/target/release/`, so `make test` builds the crate there first.

### Versions

- flutter_rust_bridge's release is pinned in `app/rust/Cargo.toml`, in `app/pubspec.yaml` for the runtime and hook packages, and, through `app/rust/Cargo.lock`, for the codegen. A bump of one alone fails `make ci`, so `.github/dependabot.yml` ignores `flutter_rust_bridge*` in the `/app` pub entry and the `/app/rust` cargo entry.
- The mover of flutter_rust_bridge is the PM: one PR bumps all three pins, regenerates the bindings, and commits what the codegen writes.
- `libc` is held at 0.2.189 in `app/rust/Cargo.lock`: with 0.2.190, `backtrace` fails to compile for iOS. `.github/dependabot.yml` ignores `libc` `= 0.2.190` in the `/app/rust` cargo entry. Upstream tracks the break in [rust-lang/libc#5601](https://github.com/rust-lang/libc/issues/5601). As of 2026-10-15 that issue is open, 0.2.190 is the latest 0.2 release, and the dyld fix ([rust-lang/libc#5606](https://github.com/rust-lang/libc/pull/5606)) is not merged.
- The ignore is dropped when a fixed `libc` release ships, after the macOS CI job passes on a PR that moves `app/rust/Cargo.lock` to it. A later release that still breaks the job is added to the ignore.
- Dependabot also proposes the bumps of `app/rust/rust-toolchain.toml`, which stays on the release of `engine/rust-toolchain.toml`.
