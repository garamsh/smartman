# Study

The `study` domain: studying one or more decks, scheduling their cards for review, and the bridge through which it calls the Rust engine.

## Current decisions

### Surface and location

- The domain lives in `app/lib/study/`; its public surface is `app/lib/study/study.dart`, with internals under `app/lib/study/src/` ([ADR 0002](adr/0002-domain-public-surface-and-shared-database.md)).
- The surface exports what the composition site constructs and overrides: `StudyRepository`, `Scheduler` and the types its reviews and `StudyRepository.record` take and give, `CardState`, `Answer`, `FuzzFactor` and `Review`, `localOffsetAt`, `studyCoachShownKey` and `dailyMinutesAskedKey`, the stored keys a test sets to start past the coach and the first-run daily minutes step, and the providers `studyRepositoryProvider`, `schedulerProvider`, `studyClockProvider`, `studyZoneProvider` and `studyRandomProvider`; and what `home/` composes: `StudyScreen` and `openStudy`, the daily study minutes' `FirstDailyMinutes` and `DailyMinutesRow`, `TodayCounter`, and the count it shows, `TodayCount` with `TodayPending`, `TodayCounted`, which carries the time one card takes and the minutes the session takes, and `TodayUnavailable`, and `TodayBuilder`; `TodayOverviewReader` and what it reads, `TodayOverviewRead` with `OverviewPending`, `OverviewUnavailable` and `TodayOverview`, which carries the day's daily minutes, with `DailyGoal`; `StatsView`, the 기록 tab's content; and `ProgressCounter` with `DeckProgress` and `ProgressBuilder` ([`home.md`](home.md)).
- `Scheduler.fold` is the fold `sync` recomputes a card's state with: the card's stored reviews in `reviewed_at` order, through the engine from a new card, each with its stored fuzz factor and a null factor as none ([`sync.md`](sync.md)). The domain does not import `sync`. `Scheduler.history` is the same fold with the state after each review, which the mastered weeks read.
- The domain reads the decks' cards only through `deck`'s surface (`DeckRepository.listCards`, [`deck.md`](deck.md)).

### Session

- A session studies a `DeckSelection`: one deck from its deck screen or its row on the 오늘 tab, or every deck from the 오늘 tab's today, backlog or done card. `home/` opens `StudyScreen` through `openStudy` and builds `TodayCounter` itself ([ADR 0009](adr/0009-multi-deck-session-and-random-queue-order.md), [ADR 0039](adr/0039-name-every-top-level-folder-by-its-responsibility-compose-across-domains-in-a-home-shell-and-keep-seams-off-screens.md)).
- A study day runs from 04:00 (`studyDayStartHour`) to the next 04:00 in the device's time zone, each boundary read at the instant it names, so a day across a change of offset still starts and ends at 04:00 local time. `studyZoneProvider` gives the zone, as the offset from UTC at an instant; the composition site passes `localOffsetAt`, and tests a fixed zone ([ADR 0033](adr/0033-count-cards-left-per-study-day-from-04-00-and-continue-as-unrecorded-practice.md)).
- A day takes its new cards less the cards first answered in it, across every deck, from the stored reviews, never below zero; an unused figure is not carried to the next day. A day's new cards fill the daily study minutes the user sets, after the reviews due, at the user's own pace ([ADR 0085](adr/0085-fill-each-day-s-new-cards-from-the-daily-study-minutes-the-user-sets-stored-per-user-on-the-server-and-synced.md), `app/lib/study/src/study_time.dart`):
  - the minutes are `StudyRepository.dailyMinutes`, the stored setting, or `defaultDailyMinutes` (20) while it gives none, from `minDailyMinutes` (5) to `maxDailyMinutes` (180), any whole number between; a change applies to today at once;
  - the day takes max(0, floor((minutes × 60,000 − the reviews due × the review pace) / the first-answer pace)), in whole milliseconds, from the first day, with no cold start;
  - the reviews due are those of the day as it stood when it started, so studying in it does not move them: the cards due before the day ends not answered in it, and those answered in it that had an answer before;
  - the pace is read from the latest `studyDayWindow` (30) study days before today, a study day being one with an answer, whatever rest lies between them, each answer up to `answerCapMs` (30 s); it is kept for a review and for a card's first answer apart, each answer weighted 0.5^(age / `paceHalfLife`) (7 study days), or `defaultAnswerMs` (10 s) for a kind with fewer than `paceMinimum` (20) answers in the window ([ADR 0043](adr/0043-set-each-day-s-new-cards-from-the-user-s-recency-weighted-study-time-and-pace-and-count-an-answer-s-time-only-in-the-foreground.md));
  - `daysToStartAll` counts the study days after today until every never-reviewed card is first answered at the day's figure: none when today takes them all, the rest over the day's figure rounded up, and never when that figure is zero ([ADR 0090](adr/0090-pick-the-daily-study-minutes-as-one-large-number-between-minus-and-plus-typed-in-place-with-two-tiles-of-what-they-mean.md)).
  - `readStudyDay` gives the day with its pace, the reviews due at its start, the cards started in it and its minutes, which `readToday` passes on.

### Daily study minutes on screen

How the setting the day's new cards fill is changed: in the first-run step, and after it in settings alone; `home/` places the step and the row ([`home.md`](home.md), [ADR 0086](adr/0086-show-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-on-the-today-card-the-completion-card-a-one-time-first-run-step-the-account-screen-and-the-web-s-today-page.md), [ADR 0090](adr/0090-pick-the-daily-study-minutes-as-one-large-number-between-minus-and-plus-typed-in-place-with-two-tiles-of-what-they-mean.md), [ADR 0091](adr/0091-draw-the-today-card-as-a-progress-bar-and-change-the-daily-study-minutes-only-in-settings.md)), and `app/lib/study/src/daily_minutes_sheet.dart` holds them.

- `DailyMinutesPicker` is the picker: with `first`, an `AppSheet`, "하루 몇 분 공부할까요?", with "건너뛰기" above "저장"; otherwise a screen, `openDailyMinutesScreen`, with "하루 공부 시간" and a back button in its top bar, which saves nothing, its content in a list that scrolls above the keypad, and "저장" pinned at its foot. Both hold:
  - the minutes as one large number with "분", read as "하루 n분, 눌러서 직접 입력", between a − and a +, "5분 줄이기" and "5분 늘리기", which move them by `dailyMinuteStep` (5) within the bounds and disable at each; a step that disables while focused hands the focus to the other;
  - tapping the number turns it into a field on the number keypad, digits only, at most three: a whole number within the bounds is accepted; outside them "5분에서 180분 사이로 적어 주세요" shows under it, the field stays open and "저장" is disabled; Done or leaving the field commits it, and an empty field keeps the minutes before;
  - two tiles from `MinutesPlan`, which `readMinutesPlan` reads over every deck (`app/lib/study/src/daily_minutes.dart`), following the number as it is typed: "하루 새 카드" with "약 n장", the day's figure less the cards started today, no more than those never answered; and "새 카드 모두 시작" with "오늘", "n일 뒤" by `daysToStartAll`, "새 카드를 모두 시작했어요" with none left, or "이 시간은 복습으로 차서 새 카드는 시작하지 않아요" when none fit. In a backlog, "밀린 복습을 마칠 때까지 새 카드는 쉬어요." alone. They are a live region a screen reader hears again when it changes, and the sheet scrolls so they stay above the keyboard;
  - "저장" writes the minutes through `StudyRepository.setDailyMinutes` and closes the picker, resolving to the minutes; a `SettingNotSaved` is shown in it, and it stays open.
- `askDailyMinutesOnce` shows the sheet once per device as the first-run step, "하루 몇 분 공부할까요?" with "건너뛰기", while no minutes are stored; `dailyMinutesAskedKey` in `studyStorageProvider` records that it has shown. `FirstDailyMinutes` asks when its route is on top, and `openStudy` before it pushes a `StudyScreen`.
- `DailyMinutesRow`, the account screen's row: "하루 공부 시간" with the minutes and a chevron, and no icon, which opens the picker's screen and shows the minutes saved.
- The queue is built when the session opens, from the cards of every selected deck: the cards left today. A card is due when it falls due before the day ends ([ADR 0025](adr/0025-order-a-review-backlog-by-due-date-and-pause-new-cards.md), [ADR 0033](adr/0033-count-cards-left-per-study-day-from-04-00-and-continue-as-unrecorded-practice.md)):
  - the daily load is the sum, over every reviewed card of the decks, of one over its interval in days (due time less last review), at least one;
  - the decks are in backlog when their due cards outnumber `backlogDays` (3) times the daily load;
  - in backlog, the queue is every due card, earliest due first, equal due times in the order read, and no new card; each run of `backlogBucketSize` (10) consecutive cards is shuffled in its place;
  - otherwise, the queue is every due card and as many cards never reviewed across the decks as the day has left, oldest first by creation time, shuffled together, so each new card lands at a random place among the due ones ([ADR 0009](adr/0009-multi-deck-session-and-random-queue-order.md)).
- Past today's cards the session goes on with the cards never reviewed that the queue did not take, oldest first by creation time across the decks, in that order, each answered and recorded as any other: from the completion card's "학습하기", or at once when the session opens with no card left today. A card answered today is not among them, so it comes back only as a "Don't know"'s re-show. With none left to take, the completion card says so ([ADR 0049](adr/0049-study-in-one-flow-that-goes-on-past-today-s-cards-as-recorded-study-with-the-current-card-showing-its-front-alone.md)).
- Today's list places each card of the selected decks, from its stored state and the answers of the study day, through the engine's `today`, and `dayPlanOf` (`app/lib/study/src/today.dart`) makes the day plan from it: the selection the rules above take, the cards still on "Don't know" that they did not, and the cards done ([ADR 0061](adr/0061-keep-a-card-failed-today-in-today-s-list-until-it-is-passed-through-a-pure-engine-function-over-the-day-s-reviews.md), [ADR 0062](adr/0062-take-the-study-queue-and-the-top-bar-s-count-from-today-s-list-so-a-reopened-day-goes-on-in-the-app-and-the-web.md)):
  - the queue a session opens with is the selection, ordered as above, with each card still on "Don't know" put back as a re-show is, after a gap drawn from the session's random source or at the end, in a backlog as out of one;
  - the top bar counts done out of the day's cards, those answered today within the day's plan before the session opened and the queue it opened with, each once; done is those whose latest answer today, stored or the session's own as it stands, is "Know"; beside it, "추가 k장" counts the cards answered past the plan, apart ([ADR 0087](adr/0087-count-the-cards-studied-past-the-day-s-plan-apart-so-they-never-reopen-the-day-or-grow-its-goal.md));
  - a session counts its own answers in the study day it opened in, whatever their time, so one open across 04:00 goes on counting; the next day's list starts at the first open after 04:00;
  - so a session reopened the same day goes on where the last left off, with a card answered "Don't know" back in its queue;
  - the cards studied past the day's plan, `StudyDay.extra`, are the cards first answered in the day, in the order answered, after the day's new cards; the plan leaves them out, neither selected, left nor done whatever their answer and due time, and holds them apart, so they never reopen the day ([ADR 0087](adr/0087-count-the-cards-studied-past-the-day-s-plan-apart-so-they-never-reopen-the-day-or-grow-its-goal.md)).
- Each shuffle is a forward Fisher–Yates over its run, the whole queue out of backlog, a draw of 0 keeping the card in its place.
- A "Don't know" puts the card back after a gap drawn from `reshowGapMin` (3) to `reshowGapMax` (5) other cards, or after as many as remain. Each re-show is a review passed to the engine.
- `studyDayStartHour`, `reshowGapMin`, `reshowGapMax`, `backlogDays`, `backlogBucketSize` and the constants of a day's new cards above are constants in `app/lib/study/src/queue.dart`. The web app applies the same rules, with the same constants, in `web/src/features/study/queue.ts` ([`web-study.md`](web-study.md)); a change to them changes both, and `web/src/features/study/__tests__/rules.test.ts` fails when one differs from the other's, or `studyDayStartHour` from the server's `study_day_start_hour()`, or the minutes' bounds from `study_settings`' check ([`server.md`](server.md)).
- Every random choice of a session comes from one `Random`, which `studyRandomProvider` makes from the session's start time. The composition site seeds it from that time; tests inject their own.
- `TodayCounter` counts the cards left today, the day plan's left cards ([ADR 0062](adr/0062-take-the-study-queue-and-the-top-bar-s-count-from-today-s-list-so-a-reopened-day-goes-on-in-the-app-and-the-web.md)): the selection a session over its decks would take now and the cards still on "Don't know", as reviews and new ones, or as reviews in a backlog, and passes the count to the builder `home/` gives it: a deck row's chip, a row of the 오늘 tab's decks, and the deck screen's today card. In a backlog the deck screen adds "밀린 복습을 마칠 때까지 새 카드는 쉬어요.", and the 오늘 tab shows its backlog card ([`home.md`](home.md)). `readToday` reads the cards, their states, the study day, today's answers, the day plan and the count once, for `TodayCounter` and `TodayOverviewReader` alike. It reads the count when it is built, when its decks change, and when its route comes back on top; a failed read is passed on as its localized message.
- `TodayOverviewReader` reads, for every deck, what the 오늘 tab shows ([ADR 0036](adr/0036-organise-the-app-in-today-decks-and-record-tabs-and-define-the-daily-goal-forecast-and-card-progress.md)), each from `app/lib/study/src/overview.dart` or the queue's own rules:
  - the count, as `TodayCounter` reads it;
  - the daily goal (`DailyGoal`), from the day plan: done is the plan's cards answered "Know" last today, and the goal adds the cards left, so a card answered "Don't know" is not done until it is answered "Know";
  - the cards studied past the day's plan, as "오늘 n장 더 했어요", never in the goal;
  - where the user stands today (`TodayStatus.of`), the first that holds over the goal and the streak: nothing done with a streak of two days or more, nothing done, three cards or fewer left, half of the goal or more done, some done ([ADR 0046](adr/0046-state-where-the-user-stands-today-on-the-today-card-in-place-of-the-goal-s-numbers.md));
  - the streak, `streak` over the answers since the day began and `studyDaysBefore`, as the completion card counts it;
  - the next review (`nextReviewOf`): the first study day after today, within `chartDays` (7), on which a stored state falls due, each day from 04:00 to 04:00 in the device's zone, with how many cards fall due in it;
  - the last seven study days (`lastWeek`), today last: each day's cards, each once, and its answers' time, each up to `answerCapMs`, from the answers stored since the first of them ([ADR 0088](adr/0088-show-the-last-seven-study-days-on-the-today-tab-and-page-in-place-of-the-next-seven-days-forecast.md));
  - in a backlog, the catch-up (`catchUp`): the overdue reviews left at the start of each of the seven days from today when the user clears the average daily load a day, `dailyLoad` over the decks' stored states rounded up and at least one.
- A card is mastered (익힘) when its interval, its due time less its last review, is at least `masteredInterval` (21 days), learning (학습 중) when reviewed below it, and new (새 카드) without a stored state (`progressOf`). `ProgressCounter` counts a selection's cards by progress (`DeckProgress`) for the deck rows and the deck screen. Both counters read again when their decks change and when their route comes back on top.
- The 기록 tab's metrics are functions in `app/lib/study/src/stats.dart`, read together by `readStats` from the device's database and the engine ([ADR 0037](adr/0037-define-the-record-tab-s-retention-recall-bands-mastered-weeks-and-study-days-and-compute-them-on-the-device.md)), which `StatsView` shows:
  - the retention (`retentionByDay`): for each study day, the scheduled reviews answered "Know" over the scheduled reviews, a review being scheduled when it is a card's first answer of the day and the card has an answer on an earlier day; a period's value (`retentionOver`) sums its days, and its gap is the share less `targetRetention` (0.9);
  - the recall bands (`RecallBands`): the engine's probability of recall now (`Scheduler.retrievability`) of each card of the decks with a stored state, from `strongRecall` (0.95) strong, from `fadingRecall` (0.8) fair, below it fading, with the mean;
  - the mastered weeks (`masteredByWeek`): at the end of each of the last `masteredWeeks` (8) weeks of seven study days, the last ending with today's, the cards of the decks whose state from their reviews before then is mastered;
  - the study days (`studyDayCounts`): the distinct cards answered on each of the last `heatmapDays` (84) study days, with the date each starts on;
  - the current streak, `streak` over every stored answer, and the best (`bestStreak`), the longest run of study days with one.
  - The retention, the study days and the streaks count every stored answer, a deleted card's included; the bands and the weeks count only the cards of the decks. An answer past today's cards is stored as any other, so it is in each.

### Storage

- The `reviews` and `card_states` tables in `app/lib/database/database.dart` belong to this domain. `card_states` holds each reviewed card's stability, difficulty, last review and due time; a new card has no row. Schema v2 added it ([ADR 0005](adr/0005-study-queue-and-card-state.md)).
- Every answer of the day's cards writes one `reviews` row (`good` for "Know", `again` for "Don't know", with its time, duration on screen with the app resumed and fuzz factor, which the answer draws from the session's `Random`). Schema v3 added `fuzz_factor`, checked to `[0, 1)`; a row written before it holds null, as its review was not fuzzed ([ADR 0012](adr/0012-fuzz-review-intervals.md)).
- Each write, after an answer or a toggle, replays the card's answers in this session as they stand, from its state when the session opened, one engine review per answer with that answer's fuzz factor. It then rewrites those `reviews` rows, keeping their ids, times and factors, and the card's `card_states` row, in one transaction ([ADR 0007](adr/0007-study-swipe-screen-and-session-toggle.md)). Each row it writes takes the write's time as `updated_at` and is left unsynced; sync pushes it, the server keeping a review's rating by the later `updated_at` ([`sync.md`](sync.md)).
- Schema v4 added `updated_at`, `deleted_at` and `dirty` to both tables; schema v7 dropped `deleted_at` from both, since nothing wrote it ([ADR 0077](adr/0077-drop-the-review-and-card-state-tombstones-that-nothing-writes.md), [`sync.md`](sync.md)).
- The engine calls and the write run after the screen has changed, chained in the order given.
- `StudyRepository` receives its id generator, and the session its clock (`studyClockProvider`), zone (`studyZoneProvider`) and random source (`studyRandomProvider`), so tests fix all four.
- When a session opens, and when a count is read, it also reads, through `StudyRepository`, for the day's new cards, the completion line and the streak; deleted reviews are left out, and a review counts whether or not its card is live ([`sync.md`](sync.md)):
  - every answer since the day began, and, for each card answered since then, its latest answer before, through `reviews_card_id_reviewed_at`;
  - every stored card state, across every deck;
  - the window's first study day, walking back one study day at a time through the latest answer before each, one probe of `reviews_reviewed_at` per day, then the window's answers, each with whether it is its card's first live answer, one probe of `reviews_card_id_reviewed_at` each;
  - for each earlier study day, back from the day before, the latest answer before the day ends, one probe of `reviews_reviewed_at` per day, stopping at the first day without one ([ADR 0034](adr/0034-key-synced-rows-by-client-made-uuids-purge-tombstones-past-a-revision-horizon-and-index-each-query.md) §4).
  - Each of these reads is SQL comparing the stored text, so it reaches its index; drift's own comparison of times would go through `julianday()`.
- The `study_settings` table (schema 9) belongs to this domain too: at most one row, the daily minutes with their edit time and `dirty`, which `StudyRepository.setDailyMinutes` writes unsynced and `sync` pushes and merges ([ADR 0085](adr/0085-fill-each-day-s-new-cards-from-the-daily-study-minutes-the-user-sets-stored-per-user-on-the-server-and-synced.md), [`sync.md`](sync.md)). No row reads as the default.
- `readStats` reads every review, oldest first, whatever its card, and the decks' cards with their states through `readCards`.

### The bridge to the engine

- The app calls the engine ([`engine.md`](engine.md)) through flutter_rust_bridge 2.13.0, built with its native-assets backend ([ADR 0004](adr/0004-flutter-rust-bridge.md)).
- The bridge crate is `rust_lib_smartman` in `app/rust/`. It depends on `engine/` by path and declares the Dart surface in `app/rust/src/api/engine.rs`:
  - `review_new_card` — a new card's first review, with an optional fuzz factor;
  - `review_card` — a review of a card from its persisted state parts, with an optional fuzz factor;
  - `replay` — a history folded into a state;
  - `retrievability` — the probability of recall at a time;
  - `todays_list` — called synchronously, as it is pure and cheap: each card's place in a study day's list, `Today.absent`, `Today.left` or `Today.done`, from its due time and reviews, in the order given ([ADR 0061](adr/0061-keep-a-card-failed-today-in-today-s-list-until-it-is-passed-through-a-pure-engine-function-over-the-day-s-reviews.md)). ([`engine.md`](engine.md))
- Times cross the bridge as whole seconds since the Unix epoch, as the engine holds them. Answers cross as the app's two buttons, `Answer.know` and `Answer.dontKnow`.
- The generated Dart bindings are in `app/lib/study/src/rust/`; only this domain imports them.
- `Scheduler.load()` runs `RustLib.init()`, which loads the library, and returns the scheduler that calls it. The composition site awaits it before `runApp`.

### Failures

- Every engine `Error` variant arrives in Dart as one variant of the sealed `EngineError`, thrown by the call that was refused. `EngineError.unknownRating` cannot arise from this surface, which takes answers, not rating numbers.
- The bridge code itself does not panic: `app/rust/src/api/engine.rs` denies `panic!`, `unwrap`, `expect` and indexing, and `make lint` runs clippy over it. A panic inside flutter_rust_bridge's generated code reaches Dart as its `PanicException`, as an unexpected failure.
- The domain's expected failures are the sealed `StudyFailure`:
  - `SchedulingFailure` wraps an `EngineError`, thrown by `Scheduler`;
  - `AnswerNotSaved` is a failed write of an answer, `SettingNotSaved` one of the daily minutes, and `StudyStorageFailure` a failed read, thrown by `StudyRepository`.
- A failure of a write reaches one handler, which the study screen registers with the session: a localized snack bar on the app's messenger, shown even if the screen has closed. Any other error a write raises goes to `FlutterError.reportError`, and the writes queued after it still run. A failure reading the queue is shown in place of the session; any other error there propagates.

### Build and generated code

- `flutter build` builds the crate through the hook in `app/hook/build.dart`, with the Rust release pinned in `app/rust/rust-toolchain.toml`, for every target Flutter asks for. No Android or iOS build file names the crate.
- `app/flutter_rust_bridge.yaml` configures the codegen. Its output is committed: `app/rust/src/frb_generated.rs` and `app/lib/study/src/rust/`, including the `freezed` part file of `EngineError`. `check-generated.sh` reruns the codegen, at the release `app/rust/Cargo.lock` pins, and fails when the output differs.
- To change the surface: edit `app/rust/src/api/`, run `flutter_rust_bridge_codegen generate` in `app/`, and commit what it writes.

### Versions

- flutter_rust_bridge's release is pinned in `app/rust/Cargo.toml`, in `app/pubspec.yaml` for the runtime and hook packages, and, through `app/rust/Cargo.lock`, for the codegen. A bump of one alone fails `make ci`, so `.github/dependabot.yml` ignores `flutter_rust_bridge*` in the `/app` pub entry and the `/app/rust` cargo entry.
- The mover of flutter_rust_bridge is the PM: one PR bumps all three pins, regenerates the bindings, and commits what the codegen writes.
- `libc` is held at 0.2.189 in `app/rust/Cargo.lock`: with 0.2.190, `backtrace` fails to compile for iOS. `.github/dependabot.yml` ignores `libc` `= 0.2.190` in the `/app/rust` cargo entry. Upstream tracks the break in [rust-lang/libc#5601](https://github.com/rust-lang/libc/issues/5601). As of 2026-10-15 that issue is open, 0.2.190 is the latest 0.2 release, and the dyld fix ([rust-lang/libc#5606](https://github.com/rust-lang/libc/pull/5606)) is not merged.
- The ignore is dropped when a fixed `libc` release ships, after the macOS CI job passes on a PR that moves `app/rust/Cargo.lock` to it. A later release that still breaks the job is added to the ignore.
- Dependabot also proposes the bumps of `app/rust/rust-toolchain.toml`, which stays on the release of `engine/rust-toolchain.toml`.
