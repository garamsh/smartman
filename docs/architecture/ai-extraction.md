# AI extraction

The `extract-cards` Edge Function that turns a photo of a study page, a vocabulary page or notes, into candidate cards, its limits and privacy, how its model is switched, the eval that chooses the model, and the app's `photo_import` domain that calls it.

## Current decisions

### Location

- The function is `supabase/functions/extract-cards/`, a Deno module with no third-party dependency: it calls Supabase Auth, PostgREST and OpenRouter with `fetch` ([ADR 0013](adr/0013-extract-cards-function-and-eval.md)).
- It reads the caller through `supabase/functions/_shared/auth.ts`, which the `delete-account` function shares ([ADR 0026](adr/0026-delete-the-account-through-an-edge-function.md)); a deploy bundles it.
- `index.ts` is its composition site: it reads the configuration once and passes `fetch`, the logger and the clock to the handler, which constructs none of them.
- The eval is `tools/eval/`, outside the function, so the deployed folder holds only the function. It imports the function's extraction module, so it measures the same prompt, schema and single call.
- Merging the pull request that changes the function deploys it, through Supabase's GitHub integration; a change needing a new secret or changing the model or the cost of a call merges only after the owner approves it ([`server.md`](server.md) §Edge Function deploys).
- Its secrets are set by hand on the production project, by the owner or the PM only; a merge sets none.

### Contract

- `POST` with the user's access token as `Authorization: Bearer`, and a JSON body:
  - `image` — one JPEG, PNG or WebP image, base64-encoded, at most 6 MiB decoded. The type is read from the image's leading bytes; a declared type is ignored.
  - `languages` — optional `front` and `back` BCP 47 tags, the deck's languages, passed to the model as hints.
- Success is `200` with `{ "cards": [...] }`, 1 to 100 cards, each text field plain trimmed text ([ADR 0051](adr/0051-extract-each-card-with-a-question-a-fullest-and-a-short-answer-and-a-detail-step-and-eval-notes-pages-by-terms.md)):
  - `kind` — `vocabulary` for an entry of a vocabulary list, glossary, bilingual table or dictionary page, `note` for a point of notes;
  - `type` — what the card asks ([ADR 0063](adr/0063-ask-prompt-v6-for-atomic-typed-cards-and-a-vocabulary-entry-s-senses-and-eval-long-answers-and-senses.md), [ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)): `concept`, a named term, concept, rule or exception and its statement, which every vocabulary card and every judgment card is; `cloze`, a line or sentence of the page with each word to recall written `___`, and its `cloze`; `identify`, an example sentence the page prints with the word to judge in single quotes, and its category and reason; or `table`, a whole table of the page with the cells to recall as blanks, and its `table`. Only a note is a `cloze` or a `table` card;
  - `front` — the headword or phrase as the page prints it, or, for a note, the name of the concept, rule or exception it states, never a question, 1 to 500 characters (code points);
  - `answer` — a note's theory, the definition, rule or exception, in a sentence or a few, 1 to 1000 characters; on a vocabulary card, composed from its senses: one sense's meaning, or one line per sense with its labels, each in brackets, before its meaning; on a cloze card, composed from its answers, one per line; on a table card, composed from its table: the column headers on the first line, then one line per row, cells joined by ` | `;
  - `short` — the `answer` again, and `step` — 1, for an app that offers cards by step on a slider ([ADR 0052](adr/0052-choose-a-photo-s-cards-and-how-full-their-answers-are-on-a-five-step-slider-in-the-app.md)); the model writes neither, and the function fills both ([ADR 0058](adr/0058-stop-asking-the-model-for-a-short-form-and-a-step-with-prompt-v5-and-add-a-notes-sample-with-the-writer-s-additions.md));
  - `topic` — the name of the page section the card comes from, its heading without numbering, or the page's title where it has no headings, 1 to 60 characters; cards of one section share it, so an app can group them by it ([ADR 0055](adr/0055-cover-everything-a-notes-page-teaches-at-step-5-with-prompt-v4-and-eval-a-dense-page-by-coverage-per-page.md));
  - `examples` — present only when the page prints any for the card, 1 to 5 example sentences, phrases or worked examples of 1 to 500 characters each; a note's worked examples are those the page prints for its rule;
  - `senses` — on a vocabulary card, its meanings in the page's order, 1 to 10, each `{labels, meaning, examples}` ([ADR 0076](adr/0076-ask-prompt-v8-for-each-sense-s-printed-labels-and-examples-transcribed-never-inferred-and-eval-labels-on-pages-that-print-none.md), the shape [ADR 0075](adr/0075-store-a-vocabulary-card-s-senses-as-a-typed-block-and-show-each-label-in-brackets-before-its-meaning.md) states for the app):
    - `labels` — 0 to 5, the labels the page prints for that meaning, in its order, transcribed and never inferred, so none where the page prints none, and none from a part of speech printed elsewhere, such as before each reading of a pronunciation: a part of speech `{"kind": "part_of_speech", "category": "<name>", "text": "..."}`, its category one of `noun`, `verb`, `adjective`, `adverb`, `pronoun`, `preposition`, `conjunction`, `interjection`, `determiner`, `numeral`, `particle`, `auxiliary`, `classifier` and `phrase`, or any other label `{"kind": "other", "category": null, "text": "..."}`, such as a semantic class; `text` as printed, without the brackets around it, 1 to 500 characters;
    - `meaning` — the translation or the definition as printed, 1 to 500 characters;
    - `examples` — 0 to 5, the sentences the page prints under that meaning, each 1 to 500 characters;
  - `extras` — on a vocabulary card, present only when the page prints any, 1 to 10 `{label, text}` for anything else it prints for the entry as a whole under its own label, such as 유의어, 반의어 or 파생어, and the grammar the parts of speech do not hold, such as a gender, a plural or a past form, verbatim (ADR 0076);
  - `pronunciation` — on a vocabulary card, present only when the page prints one, verbatim, a pronunciation per part of speech included, such as `명 ['kantent] / 형 [kan'tent]`, 1 to 500 characters;
  - `meaning` — a vocabulary card's `answer`, for apps that read it ([ADR 0028](adr/0028-extract-each-card-in-fields-and-compose-its-answer-at-a-detail-level-in-the-app.md)); a note card has none;
  - `partOfSpeech` — on a vocabulary card of one sense with labels, those labels, each in brackets, for apps that read it; absent with several senses, whose labels are in `answer`;
  - `table` — on a table card only, its table as the page prints it ([ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)): `columns`, 1 to 8 column headers, the top-left corner included; `rows`, 1 to 20 rows of exactly as many cells, each starting with its row header; and `blanks`, the cells to recall, one or more distinct `{row, column}` counted from 0 within `rows`, each on a cell that holds text. A header or cell the page leaves empty is `""`, and each is at most 500 characters;
  - `cloze` — on a cloze card only, `{answers}`: what fills each `___` of its front, in order, 1 to 10 of 1 to 500 characters;
  - `back` — for apps that read only `front` and `back`: a vocabulary card's `partOfSpeech`, a space and `meaning`, or `meaning` alone, so several senses read one per line; a note card's `answer`, so a table card's is its table as text and a cloze card's its answers.
- An app that has not updated reads a vocabulary card as before and a note card as its `front` and `back`, ignoring `topic`, `type`, `senses`, `extras` and `pronunciation`, the fullest answer at every level: its parser, `app/lib/photo_import/src/extraction_client.dart` as released before topics, limits no field's length, ignores the keys it does not know, and takes `back` as the meaning when there is none. Today's parser refuses a `topic` over 60 characters, and refuses a card whose `type` it does not know, so a response holding a `table` card fails its whole extraction. The function therefore deploys prompt v7 only once an app that reads `type: table` is in use, #212 part 1 ([ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)).
- Prompt v8's senses deploy before #254, the app change that reads them: an app built from `main` before #254 reads each sense's `meaning` and no label, and #254's app refuses the `{pos, meaning}` sense of prompt v7, so it merges right after this change ([ADR 0076](adr/0076-ask-prompt-v8-for-each-sense-s-printed-labels-and-examples-transcribed-never-inferred-and-eval-labels-on-pages-that-print-none.md)).
- Every refusal is `{ "error": "<code>" }`, the code naming the case the app shows:

| Status | Code | When |
|---|---|---|
| 400 | `bad_request` | the body is not JSON, has no base64 `image`, or has a malformed `languages` |
| 401 | `unauthenticated` | no bearer token, an invalid one, the anon key, or an anonymous sign-in |
| 403 | `suspended` | an admin suspended the user ([ADR 0072](adr/0072-suspend-a-user-through-supabase-auth-s-ban-and-restrictive-write-policies-remove-a-public-deck-from-sharing-for-good-and-read-any-deck-in-the-admin-area.md)) |
| 405 | `method_not_allowed` | a method other than `POST` |
| 413 | `too_large` | the image is over 6 MiB decoded, or the body is larger than such an image could make it |
| 415 | `unsupported_type` | the image is not JPEG, PNG or WebP |
| 422 | `no_cards` | the model found no card on the page |
| 429 | `rate_limited` | the user has made today's extractions (UTC) on its plan; the body also holds `daily_limit`, the plan's limit |
| 502 | `model_error` | OpenRouter failed, or the output was invalid twice |
| 503 | `not_configured` | `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` or a platform variable is missing, or the caller's plan has no settings or names a malformed model ID |

- A request is checked in this order: method, configuration, user, body, suspension, the plan with its daily limit, model. A refused request never reaches the model, and one refused before the plan never reaches the count.
- `config.toml` sets `verify_jwt = true` for the function, so the platform refuses a request without a valid JWT first. The function then asks Supabase Auth for the token's user, which refuses the anon key, and refuses a user with `is_anonymous`.

### Model call

- OpenRouter chat completions, with the image as a data URL and `response_format` a strict JSON schema of the card list, generated from the same limits the parser enforces (`cards.ts`).
- `provider.require_parameters` routes only to providers that honour the schema; `max_tokens` is 16384.
- The schema asks for `kind`, `type`, `front`, `answer`, `topic`, `senses`, `extras`, `pronunciation`, `examples`, `table` and `cloze` on every card, and for no `short`, `step`, `meaning`, `partOfSpeech` or `back`, which the parser composes. Strict structured outputs require every key: `kind` and `type` are enums, a vocabulary, cloze or table card's `answer` and a note's `pronunciation` are `null`, a note's `senses` and `extras` and absent examples are empty lists, a sense without a label or an example has empty `labels` or `examples`, another label's `category` is `null`, `table` and `cloze` are `null` on every card but their own type, and the parser turns an absent value into an absent field. A table's row and column are integers the schema does not bound; the parser checks them.
- The prompt is `prompt_v8.ts` ([ADR 0076](adr/0076-ask-prompt-v8-for-each-sense-s-printed-labels-and-examples-transcribed-never-inferred-and-eval-labels-on-pages-that-print-none.md)): prompt v7 ([ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)) with each sense's printed labels, transcribed and never inferred, and its own examples, and grammar printed for the entry as a whole in `extras`. Prompt v7 is prompt v6 ([ADR 0063](adr/0063-ask-prompt-v6-for-atomic-typed-cards-and-a-vocabulary-entry-s-senses-and-eval-long-answers-and-senses.md)), itself prompt v5 ([ADR 0058](adr/0058-stop-asking-the-model-for-a-short-form-and-a-step-with-prompt-v5-and-add-a-notes-sample-with-the-writer-s-additions.md)) with atomic, exam-ready cards and a `type` on each, with a whole table on one table card, a cloze of one or more blanks, exception cards that state what the exception does, and examples only from the page. The model's instructions are the prompt version the server serves the caller, saved and activated in the web admin area ([ADR 0073](adr/0073-keep-the-extraction-prompt-as-append-only-versions-in-the-database-served-by-an-active-and-an-admin-only-test-label-and-record-what-served-each-call.md), [`server.md`](server.md) §Prompt store): the active version, or for an admin the test version while one is set. Version 1, the seed, is `prompt_v8.ts`'s `SYSTEM_PROMPT`, and a new prompt is a saved version, not a new file, so a prompt change reaches users when an admin activates it, not on merge. The schema, the parser and the user turn with its language hints stay in the function, so a version that breaks the schema fails as invalid output and saves no card.
- For notes, cards hold the theory and the exceptions, each card one fact, small enough to recall and grade at once:
  - one card per fact, never a second card asking it again in another form; an answer that would list more than 5 items is split into several cards;
  - every definition and every rule on its own card, its front a short keyword naming the concept, such as "광합성" or "ㄷ 불규칙 (듣다)", never a question, and its answer the theory;
  - a list line, a group's name followed by its members, as a cloze card: its front the whole line with each member written `___` and its `cloze` the members in order, never a concept card answering the list; a line of more than 5 members as several cloze cards of at most 5 blanks, each keeping the group's name;
  - a cloze also for a sentence recalled in context that has no concept card of its own: one card with a blank for each word to recall, never asking what a concept or table card answers;
  - every line under a point, a member, case, condition or variant of its rule, covered by a card of its own or a cloze blank, never dropped or left only as an example, its card naming the rule it belongs to;
  - a table whose cells are to be memorised, such as a classification grid, as one table card: its front the table's name, its headers kept and the cells to recall as its blanks, never one card per cell or per row;
  - a judgment card for a rule that tells A from B, its front "A vs B" and its answer the test;
  - every tip, trap or note the writer added by hand, and every line marked as important, on its own card whose front starts with "★ ";
  - each exception on its own card, its answer stating what the exception does, as the page prints it, never only that it is an exception (#188);
  - a worked example never a concept, cloze or table card: it is an example of the card whose rule it illustrates, and only when the page prints it;
  - an example copied whole and character for character, only when the page prints it for that card, never shortened, completed, extended, translated or made up, left out when it cannot be read with confidence, and none when the model cannot see one printed for the card;
  - identify cards from the example sentences the page prints for a rule that sorts words into categories, at most 3 a rule, from printed sentences only;
  - no separate rules merged, no rule or exception left out as minor, and at most 100 cards, a page over the cap keeping its definitions, rules, judgment cards, handwritten tips and marked exceptions before its table and cloze cards, and those before its identify cards.
- Nothing is invented: the prompt asks for vocabulary exactly as written; for notes it lets the model name a concept and join or shorten the page's words, and forbids any fact, meaning, label or example the page does not show. The eval measures how often a model invents anyway.
- The reply is parsed, not trusted: anything other than JSON of exactly the schema's shape, after trimming each field, is invalid output — a field empty once trimmed or over its limit, a topic over 60 characters, a key outside the schema such as v4's `short` or `step`, an unknown kind, a missing or unknown type, more than 5 examples, 10 senses or 10 extras, or more than 100 cards among it, or a sense with more than 5 labels or 5 examples, a label of an unknown kind, a part of speech whose category is not on the list, or another label with a category, the old `{pos, meaning}` sense included — and so is a vocabulary card with an answer, no sense, a table or a cloze, or of type `table` or `cloze`; a note with senses, extras or a pronunciation; a concept or identify note without an answer or with a table or a cloze; a table card without its table, with an answer or a cloze; and a cloze card without its cloze, with an answer or a table. A table is invalid with no column or row, more than 8 columns or 20 rows, a row not as wide as the columns, no blank, or a blank outside the table, on an empty cell or given twice; a cloze is invalid with no answer, more than 10, or a number of answers other than its front's `___` blanks.
- One call per photo: invalid output is `502` at once, and so is an HTTP or network failure ([ADR 0071](adr/0071-give-each-user-a-plan-whose-model-and-daily-limit-an-admin-sets-in-the-database-and-make-one-model-call-per-photo.md)). On the PM's eval run of #225's head, every first call of Claude Haiku 5.5 over the 55 samples returned valid output.
- A valid empty list is `422`.

### Switching the model

- The model is the caller's plan's, read from the database at each call, and an admin sets each plan's model in the web admin area ([ADR 0071](adr/0071-give-each-user-a-plan-whose-model-and-daily-limit-an-admin-sets-in-the-database-and-make-one-model-call-per-photo.md), [`server.md`](server.md) §Plans, [`web.md`](web.md)). No secret names a model: `CARD_MODEL` and `CARD_RETRY_MODEL` are no longer read, and the PM removes both after this change deploys.
- The plans are Free, the default, Pro and Special. A user without a grant is on Free, and so is one whose Special has ended. The migration seeds all three on `anthropic/claude-haiku-5.5` and 30 photos a day, the model and the limit production ran on 2026-10-19, when the PM had set both secrets to that model (#224).
- Earlier: on 2026-10-18 the owner first chose Claude Sonnet 5.5 from the notes report (issue #184), then moved back to Claude Haiku 5.5 the same day on a run over the 24 vocabulary pages, Haiku F1 99.0 at $0.0009 per image against Sonnet's 100.0 at $0.0161 (`docs/eval/extract-cards.md`).
- `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is a Supabase secret. Locally it is in `supabase/functions/.env`, which git ignores; creating that file is an owner action.

### Privacy

- The image is sent to OpenRouter and nowhere else: it is not stored, not written to disk, and not logged.
- Each request logs one JSON line with exactly `user_id`, `plan` (the caller's, or null when it was not read), `model` (the call's), `prompt_version` (the id of the version the call was made with, or null when none was made), `latency_ms`, `prompt_tokens`, `completion_tokens`, and `outcome` (the response code, or `ok`).
- Nothing else is printed: not the cards, the token, or a configuration value. An unexpected failure logs its line with outcome `internal` and propagates to the runtime, which answers `500`.

### Daily limit

- Each plan's daily limit of extractions per user per UTC day, counted in `public.extraction_usage` ([`server.md`](server.md)).
- The function calls `public.begin_extraction` with the service-role key, before calling the model: it resolves the caller's plan and prompt and returns the model, the limit and the prompt version, and records the extraction when it is under the limit, with the plan, model and version that serve it, so a failed or empty extraction still counts ([ADR 0073](adr/0073-keep-the-extraction-prompt-as-append-only-versions-in-the-database-served-by-an-active-and-an-admin-only-test-label-and-record-what-served-each-call.md)).
- The count and its insert run under a per-user advisory lock, so two concurrent requests cannot both take the last extraction. `public.start_extraction` and `public.consume_extraction` take the same lock and count the same rows, so a function deployed before the prompt store or before plans counts each photo once with one deployed after.

### Tests

- `supabase/functions/extract-cards/*_test.ts`, run by `make test` through `deno.sh`, with Supabase and OpenRouter replaced by a fake `fetch`: no network in CI.
- They cover authentication, size and type refusal, each kind's fields and `back`, a vocabulary card's senses, extras and pronunciation and the answer composed from one sense or several, a note card without `meaning`, a sense without a label, a sense's labels and examples, a broken label refused, a label's brackets dropped, the kinds, each card type and a missing or unknown one, a table card's table and a cloze card's answers with the answer composed from each, a malformed table or cloze refused, which type may carry a table or a cloze, the short form and step the function fills, the fields each kind may fill, the field limits, prompt v2's and v5's shapes refused, the prompt's request to cover a notes page exhaustively while inventing nothing, prompt v8's rules, its table and cloze cards, its exceptions, its examples only from the page, its type, its vocabulary form, its labels transcribed and never inferred, its examples per sense and its grammar in extras, the schema and the one call, with invalid output refused after it, the model of each plan, the daily limit of a plan stated in its refusal, a plan without settings or with a malformed model refused before any call, the configuration without a model secret, the served prompt version's text as the system prompt and its id in the log, a served row without its text or version failing before any model call, and the log line with the plan. They run without write or network permission, and a test asserts that, so a write anywhere fails the suite.
- `prompt_seed_test.ts` compares the prompt store's seed in its migration with `prompt_v8.ts`'s `SYSTEM_PROMPT`, character for character; `deno.sh` grants the tests read access to that migration besides `tools/eval/`.
- The SQL side of the limit is tested by `supabase/tests/extraction_usage_test.sql`, and the prompt store's by `supabase/tests/prompts_test.sql`.

### Eval

- `tools/eval/run.ts` runs every configuration in `tools/eval/candidates.json` over every sample and writes `docs/eval/extract-cards.md`: per configuration, for vocabulary pages precision, recall, F1, sense precision and recall, label precision and recall, category accuracy, unprinted labels, examples precision, sense examples, extras and the invented rate, overall and per page; for notes pages coverage, unsupported cards, invented examples, long answers, bare exceptions, table blanks and cloze blanks, and each notes page's coverage with the completion tokens its calls used against `max_tokens`; p50 and p90 latency, cost per image, and total spend, then the recommendation ([ADR 0051](adr/0051-extract-each-card-with-a-question-a-fullest-and-a-short-answer-and-a-detail-step-and-eval-notes-pages-by-terms.md)).
- It runs prompt v8 by default. `--prompt-version <id>` runs a version saved in the admin area, read through `public.prompt_version` with `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, and the ledger and the report name the run's prompt `version <id>` ([ADR 0073](adr/0073-keep-the-extraction-prompt-as-append-only-versions-in-the-database-served-by-an-active-and-an-admin-only-test-label-and-record-what-served-each-call.md)).
- A candidate names patterns, not IDs. At run time each resolves to the newest model in OpenRouter's model list that matches, takes images, and supports structured outputs, and the report records the exact IDs and prices.
- `candidates.json` holds one configuration, Claude Haiku 5.5 alone, the model prompts v7 and v8 are measured on ([ADR 0069](adr/0069-ask-prompt-v7-for-whole-table-and-cloze-cards-as-structured-fields-exception-cards-that-state-what-they-do-and-examples-only-from-the-page.md)). It replaces [ADR 0059](adr/0059-keep-one-sonnet-call-per-photo-after-measuring-an-outline-then-fill-pipeline-and-the-newest-models.md)'s eight configurations, which compared models. Each configuration names one model, since the function makes one call per photo; a configuration naming a retry is refused ([ADR 0071](adr/0071-give-each-user-a-plan-whose-model-and-daily-limit-an-admin-sets-in-the-database-and-make-one-model-call-per-photo.md)).
- Vocabulary pages: scores are per image, then averaged. A candidate card matches when its front and its senses' meanings, joined by "; ", or a note's `answer`, equal the ground truth's after the card key normalization ([`deck.md`](deck.md)); an image with no cards scores 0. A truth card holds its `senses`, read through the function's own sense parser, and the `examples` and `extras` its page prints for the entry as a whole (ADR 0076).
- Senses, over every candidate whose front matches an entry's after the same normalization, pooled over the vocabulary images: sense precision is the share of returned senses equal to one of the entry's in both labels and meaning, and sense recall the share of the entry's senses so returned ([ADR 0063](adr/0063-ask-prompt-v6-for-atomic-typed-cards-and-a-vocabulary-entry-s-senses-and-eval-long-answers-and-senses.md)).
- Over the matched cards of every vocabulary image, whose senses pair with the page's in order:
  - label precision is the share of returned labels whose text equals one the page prints for that sense, and label recall the share of printed labels so returned, after the same normalization;
  - category accuracy is the share of those equal labels whose kind and category are the page's too;
  - unprinted labels is the share of the senses the page prints no label for that were given one, which must be 0 (#228);
  - examples precision is the share of returned examples, on the card or a sense, that the page prints for the entry, and sense examples the share of examples printed under a sense that the returned sense holds;
  - extras is the share of printed extras returned with an equal label and text;
  - the invented rate is the share of returned fields — a sense's labels, a card's or a sense's examples, or a card's extras — where the page prints no such field;
  - each is shown as a dash when the configuration returned no such field, or the pages print none.
- The report gives these per vocabulary page too, with its F1, so a page written for one measure reads on its own (ADR 0076).
- Notes pages: a page's facts are its rules and exceptions, each holding the worked examples the page prints for it; a worked example is not a fact. A card covers a fact when its front and answer hold every one of the fact's terms after the same normalization; its examples do not count, since another rule's worked example can hold a rule's terms ([ADR 0055](adr/0055-cover-everything-a-notes-page-teaches-at-step-5-with-prompt-v4-and-eval-a-dense-page-by-coverage-per-page.md)).
  - Coverage is the share of the page's facts that some card covers, per image and then averaged. It equals coverage at step 5 in reports before prompt v5, whose cards carry no step of their own; the facts keep their `step`, which no metric reads ([ADR 0058](adr/0058-stop-asking-the-model-for-a-short-form-and-a-step-with-prompt-v5-and-add-a-notes-sample-with-the-writer-s-additions.md)).
  - Unsupported cards are the share of returned cards that cover no fact, and invented examples the share of returned examples the page does not print. A returned example counts as printed when it and one of the page's worked examples contain each other, or when a line the page prints contains it, as a rule's own worked example sits in the rule's line.
  - Long answers are the share of returned cards whose answer lists more than 5 items, counting its parts between commas, middle dots, slashes, semicolons and line breaks: the cards prompt v6's atomic rule splits ([ADR 0063](adr/0063-ask-prompt-v6-for-atomic-typed-cards-and-a-vocabulary-entry-s-senses-and-eval-long-answers-and-senses.md)). A table card is not counted, since its answer is its whole table.

### App: the `photo_import` domain

#### Photo

- The camera and the library are `image_picker`'s system camera and picker, each asked for at most 2048 px per side, without full metadata so the library needs no photo-library permission. The settings page is iOS's `app-settings:` address, opened through `url_launcher`.
- Every photo is decoded, turned upright by its EXIF orientation, scaled so its long side is at most 2048 px keeping its aspect ratio, and encoded as JPEG at quality 85, stepping down to 40 until it is at most 6 MiB. This runs on a background isolate with the pure-Dart `image` package.
- A file `image` cannot decode, such as a HEIC photo, is `PhotoUnreadable`.

#### Request and failures

- `HttpExtractionClient` posts `{"image": <base64 JPEG>}` to `<SUPABASE_URL>/functions/v1/extract-cards` with the session's access token as `Authorization: Bearer`, through `package:http` with an abortable request. Without a session it sends nothing.
- The answer is parsed at the boundary: a `200` must hold a non-empty `cards` list, each card a non-empty `front` and `back`, and a non-empty `answer`, `meaning`, `partOfSpeech`, `topic` and list of non-empty `examples` where present, the topic at most 60 characters. A vocabulary card's `senses` are each `{labels, meaning, examples}`, its labels `{kind, category, text}`, as `deck`'s `decodeSense` reads them ([ADR 0075](adr/0075-store-a-vocabulary-card-s-senses-as-a-typed-block-and-show-each-label-in-brackets-before-its-meaning.md)); any other sense shape, the function's `{pos, meaning}` before #228's PR 1 included, is `ServiceFailed`.
  - A card's answer is its `answer`, else its `meaning`, else its `back`, so a function deployed before the fields still reads.
  - The app reads no other field.
- Each failure is a `PhotoImportFailure`, shown in place of the screen with its ARB message. Its top bar's close is its one way out; the bottom bar holds what the table offers besides, and is absent where it offers nothing else ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-start-a-deck-or-a-card-from-one-sheet-that-holds-the-photo-source-and-name-public-decks-once.md)). The retake takes another photo from the photo's source, named as on the list:

| Failure | When | Offers |
|---|---|---|
| `ExtractionOffline` | the request could not reach the server | retry, retake |
| `PhotoRefused` | `413` or `415` | retake |
| `NoCardsFound` | `422` | retake |
| `DailyLimitReached` | `429`; its message states the plan's limit, "오늘 사진으로 만들 수 있는 {limit}장을 다 썼어요. 내일 다시 시도해 주세요.", when the body's `daily_limit` holds it, and the older sentence otherwise | the top bar's close |
| `ModelFailed` | `502` | retry, retake |
| `SignInRequired` | `401`, or no session | the top bar's close |
| `AccountSuspendedFailure` | `403` with the error `suspended`; its message says the account is suspended and where to write | the top bar's close |
| `ServiceFailed` | any other status, or a `200` outside the contract | retry, retake |
| `PhotoUnreadable` | the photo cannot be decoded | retake |
| `PhotoAccessDenied` | camera or photo access is refused or restricted | retake, "설정 열기"; a snack bar when the settings page does not open |
| `CameraUnavailable` | the device has no camera | "앨범에서 고르기", which picks from the album, as later retakes do |

- A `DeckFailure` while reading the deck or saving is shown in a snack bar, as the deck screens show one; `DeckDeleted` says the deck was deleted meanwhile.

## Open questions

- On Android, the system may end the app while the photo picker is open; the picked photo is then lost and the user picks again. `image_picker`'s `retrieveLostData` could recover it.
- Whether one call can cover a dense page within `max_tokens` of 16384: the proposal in ADR 0055 raises it to 32768 if a dense page uses more than 12,000 completion tokens in a call or fails twice.
- On samples 37 to 40, whether a prompt can keep the writer's numbered judgment methods as one card and a table row's verbs in its answer: under v4 and v5 alike, Sonnet wrote the methods as two cards and the verbs as examples (#191's runs, coverage 66.7 on each). Prompt v6's atomic rule asks for one fact per card, so the truth's single fact for both methods may stay uncovered under v6 by design.
- What prompt v7 covers on Claude Haiku 5.5 once rebased onto the plans of ADR 0071, and whether it brings Haiku's invented examples down: the PM re-runs the eval on the rebased head. The owner approved its cost, $0.0022 per photo against $0.0018 on v6 (ADR 0069).
- What prompt v8 scores on Claude Haiku 5.5: unprinted labels on samples 56 to 58, category accuracy, the Korean dictionary's senses, the German extras and the sense examples, against v7, and its cost per photo, which the owner approves before merge ([ADR 0076](adr/0076-ask-prompt-v8-for-each-sense-s-printed-labels-and-examples-transcribed-never-inferred-and-eval-labels-on-pages-that-print-none.md)).
- How well each model names topics, and how often it invents a fact, a part of speech or an example: the only notes report, of 2026-10-18, measured v4 before its theory-first revision.
- Which argument Google refuses in our request. Every Gemini model tried fails with it: Gemini Flash (`google/gemini-3.8-flash`) and Gemini 4 Argon with `400 INVALID_ARGUMENT`, unbilled, and Gemini 2.5 Pro with two model errors that stopped it (#189).
- The samples are rendered pages, not phone photos. Real photos can be added to `tools/eval/samples/` with a ground-truth file each.
- `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` is Supabase's legacy key form. A project that disables legacy keys would need the function to read `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEYS` instead.
