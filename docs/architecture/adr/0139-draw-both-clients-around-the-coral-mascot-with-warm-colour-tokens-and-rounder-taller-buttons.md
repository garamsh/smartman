# ADR 0139: Draw both clients around the coral mascot, with warm colour tokens and rounder, taller buttons

> Status: accepted
> Date: 2026-11-02

Append-only: once merged, the body below is not rewritten. A fact later found wrong is corrected in an appended note, not edited out; a revised decision is a new ADR that supersedes this one.

## Context

- **Issue #541.** The owner chose a generated character for the app and asked for the whole mood to follow it. The first choice, a yellow face, was replaced on 2026-11-02 by a coral blob: "코랄 말랑이로 가자". The references are Canva media `MAHZsI_Pb-k` (mascot), `MAHZsBm1XIM` (expressions) and `MAHZsLsmA4Y` (app icon). The earlier #524 mark directions are superseded.
- **The marks still drew smartman's mascot**, [ADR 0134](0134-name-the-app-knowno-serve-it-at-knowno-app-identify-it-as-app-knowno-and-reach-the-backend-at-api-knowno-app.md) having left them for later, and the colour tokens were [ADR 0027](0027-draw-both-clients-in-the-smartman-brand-and-the-v2-screens.md)'s blue-grey and navy.
- **The art is generated**, so its copyright protection may be limited. The PM's similarity check in #541 of the first coral draft found no TinEye match and no identical image on Yandex; Google Lens and trademark registers were not checked. The redraw has its own paths, and its arm is a rounded stub with no fingers.
- **The coral clashed with 모른다.** `dontKnowText` was `#C2402F`, hue 7°, and the coral `#FB7262` is hue 6°. The character's colour on a study screen would have read as an answer.
- **White on the coral is 2.7:1**, and the coral on the cream ground is 2.6:1, so the coral carries no small text on a light ground.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  - **A coral primary fill.** A darker coral under a white label is 4.5:1 only at about `#CC4A3A`, which reads as a destructive red. The true coral under a cocoa label is 5.4:1 and stays open, but needs a fill token apart from `ink`, on every screen that draws one; this change keeps the token names. The owner reviews the look in the layout stage of #541.
  - **Keep 모른다's red and make the accent another colour.** The owner chose the character, and its colour is the accent.
  - **Keep the navy for ink.** It is the old brand's and cold beside the coral.
  - **Draw the wordmark live in the font.** An SVG `<text>` depends on what the viewer has installed; outlines do not.
  - **Render the rasters with a new tool.** `sharp`, already in the web's lock file through Next.js, renders SVG; pinning it as a dev dependency adds no package.

## Decision

- **The marks**, all generated by `tools/brand/generate.mjs` from `tools/brand/mark.svg`, an original drawing, and `tools/brand/wordmarks.json`, 알아몰라 and knowno outlined from Pretendard ExtraBold:
  - the mark alone; the mark on a cream circle; the horizontal lockup, the mark on its circle beside 알아몰라 and knowno; and the stacked lockup, each lockup in a cocoa and a cream version;
  - the favicon, the web's PWA icons, the launcher icon sources and the splash sources;
  - Android's launcher icon becomes adaptive: the mark over the cream.
- **The mascot is drawn on its own cream circle** everywhere it was drawn in a tinted one, in the places [ADR 0116](0116-draw-an-icon-only-where-it-says-what-the-words-beside-it-do-not-or-follows-the-platform.md) lists. No place changes here.
- **The colours keep their names**, with new values on both clients:
  - warm cream ground, warm white surfaces and warm lines;
  - ink is cocoa, `#3B2118`, the mascot's pupil, and the primary fill stays ink, inverted in the dark theme;
  - the coral is the accent: `onHero` is the mascot's `#FB7262`, the `hero` surface is cocoa, and `brandTint` is a tint of the coral. It is never text on a light ground;
  - the dark theme is warm espresso, not navy.
- **모른다 moves to a crimson**, `dontKnowText` `#B3123F` in the light theme and `#FF8FB0` in the dark, with its tints, so it stays red and about 24° of hue from the coral. 안다's green is unchanged.
- **A button is rounder and taller**: `AppRadius.button` 18, `AppSize.button` 54, `AppSize.cta` 60, on both clients.
- **The streak flame's core** goes on being `streak` halfway toward white, now written as white, since `onHero` is no longer white.
- **Supersedes** ADR 0027's marks and colour values, and ADR 0119's button radius of 12. ADR 0119's other radii and ADR 0116's rule stand.
- **Not in this change:** the expressions on screens, and their exception to ADR 0116, and any layout. #541's later stages decide those.

## Consequences

- The screens look warm through the tokens alone, and no screen's layout moves but for the buttons' height.
- A change to the mark is one edit and `make brand`; `web.sh lint` fails on a brand file stale against it.
- `app/test/design/theme_test.dart` fails when 모른다 comes within 20° of the coral.
- The web's Tailwind tokens change with the app's, so its buttons change with them.
- The launcher icon's mark runs off the foot of the square, close up, as the reference's does.

## Signs the decision was wrong

- Users take the coral, on a chip or a blank, for a 모른다 mark.
- The crimson on a study card is read as a pink rather than as a wrong answer.
- The cocoa primary reads as heavy beside the coral, and the owner asks for the coral fill.
- The taller buttons crowd a sheet or a bottom bar on a small phone.
